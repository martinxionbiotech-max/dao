# SEO / AIO Guide

## Entity-first, not keyword-first
Priority order: search intent, entity value, source depth, originality, long-tail
opportunity, AIO potential, internal link potential, knowledge graph value.

## AIO (AI Overviews / AI Search)
Answer-first summaries, structured evidence, source provenance, historical context,
traditional interpretation, modern research, practitioner experience, uncertainty,
editorial synthesis. No keyword stuffing, no fake FAQs, no generic AI summaries.

## Indexing policy
Only index content with real informational value, sufficient context, meaningful
originality, clean language, appropriate anonymization. Thin/repetitive experiences
stay noindex or internal. Quality > page count.

## Content tiers
- S: Chinese Meditation Explained; Meditation Problems & Experiences; Chinese
  Contemplative Dictionary; Ancient Text → Modern Question; Daoist Practice
  Explained; Practitioner Experience Archive.
- A: Comparisons; Chinese Concepts in English; Classical Texts; People; Research
  Reviews; Experience Patterns.
- B: Stories; general Taoism; generic mindfulness (avoid).
