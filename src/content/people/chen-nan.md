## The man and the arc

- **The barrel-hooper who became a patriarch.** The records agree on the sharpest biographical fact and the site keeps it: 以箍桶为业 — Chen Nan made barrels for a living. The southern lineage's fourth patriarch enters the record as a craftsman, and the site's [craftsman line](/stories/cook-ding/) holds the family this belongs to: the cook, the [wheelwright](/stories/wheelwright-bian/), the bell-stand maker — the book's masters of practice all start with a trade, and the lineage's own patriarchs repeat the pattern. The trade matters here in the doctrinal sense too: his alchemy is graded workmanship (see below), not revelation.
- **The double transmission.** The records give him two teachers of two kinds: from Xue Daoguang (the former Chan master turned alchemist) he took the 太乙刀圭金丹法诀, the golden-elixir method — and the line he quoted about himself is a *text* (付我归根复命篇 — he handed me the Returning-to-the-Root chapter, a title that quotes the [ch. 16 formula](/translations/daodejing-16/) 归根复命); from a spirit-man of Limu Mountain he took the 景霄大雷琅书, the thunder-scripture. So the two great lines of the school — the inner elixir and the thunder-rites — both run through Chen Nan, and both then run onward to [Bai Yuchan](/people/bai-yuchan/), whose thunder doctrine (the method is the heart's minister; the spirits summoned are the operator's own essence) descends through this man. The site keeps the boundary on the second line: the spirit-man transmission is registered as the tradition's own account, not as history.
- **The mud pills and the name.** 常以泥土渗符水，捏成小丸救人治病，无不灵验，时人称为陈泥丸 — he mixed earth with talisman-water, kneaded little pills, healed the sick without fail, and the people called him Chen Niwan, "Chen Mud-Pill." The site registers the record as it stands: the healing claims are the tradition's claim (照录不背书, per the site's [health-claims rule](/questions/can-meditation-cure-illness/)), and the nickname is worth its weight in method — the healer is named for the *material* of his healing, dirt and water, the plainest substances in the tradition's pharmacy. His other image matches: 衣裳褴褛，终日烂醉，吟诗歌咏，出口成章 — ragged clothes, drunk all day, poems pouring out — the same mad-sage idiom the site filed for his [disciple](/people/bai-yuchan/) and for [Wang Chongyang](/people/wang-chongyang/): the display of madness as the tradition's own inverse of display.
- **The poem that names the heir.** The records carry his own verse as the transmission document: 嘉定壬申八月秋，翠虚道人在罗浮……还以金丹火候诀，说与琼山白玉蟾 — autumn of the eighth month of Jiading renshen (1212), the Cuixu man at Luofu… handing the golden-elixir fire-timing formula to Bai Yuchan of Qiongshan. The date, the place, the method, and the disciple — in the master's own poem, matching the 1212 Luofu transmission on the site's [Bai Yuchan page](/people/bai-yuchan/). The site notes the genre: a transmission poem is a document *and* a claim, and this one is the lineage's own evidence for its central hand-off.
- **The widening that made the school possible.** The Hun Yuan Lie Xian Tu record names four disciples — Ju Jiusi, Sha Daozhang, Bai Yuchan, Huang Tiangu — with the gloss the site treats as the arc's hinge: 南宗自陈楠起，门徒始众 — from Chen Nan on, the school's disciples first became many. The site's [organizer arc](/people/zhang-boduan/) holds the sequence this completes: Zhang Boduan wrote the founding text; Chen Nan widened the transmission from one-to-one to four; Bai Yuchan opened the teaching seat. The site's [Bai Yuchan page](/people/bai-yuchan/) said "he ended the one-to-one transmission" — and this page files the finer record: the ending began here, with four names instead of one.
- **The graded alchemy.** His systematic mark is the three-grade classification the records preserve: 下品地仙之道 (the earth-immortal way — the older Zhong-Lü methods of swallowing and rubbing), 中品水仙之道 (the water-immortal way — spirit and breath as the lead and mercury, the Ziting Jing), 上品天仙之道 (the heaven-immortal way) — and the doctrine that underwrites the whole: 气之根本凝成神，方曰无为而通灵 — when the root of breath condenses into spirit, only then is it called non-doing and reaching the spirit. The site's [essence-breath-spirit page](/glossary/jing-qi-shen/) and [qi page](/glossary/qi/) hold the triad this runs on, and the site registers the doctrinal point against the sequence debates elsewhere: Chen Nan's 无为 (non-doing) is *earned* — it arrives at the top of a graded curriculum, not as its beginning. A barrel-hooper's theology: the product comes after the stages.
- **Why he belongs here.** He is the site's bridge-figure: backwards to [Zhang Boduan](/people/zhang-boduan/) (the text), forwards to [Bai Yuchan](/people/bai-yuchan/) (the school), and sideways to the whole craftsman-family of the book (the trade, the graded work, the plain materials). The site files him with the [teacher question](/questions/do-i-need-a-teacher/) in view: this is what the lineage means by transmission — a poem naming a date and a disciple, a method handed over like a trade, and a school that widened only when there were four names to carry it.
