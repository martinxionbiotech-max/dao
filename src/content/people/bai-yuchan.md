## The man and the arc

- **The prodigy from the island.** The records agree on the frame and the site keeps it: born in Qiongzhou — Hainan, the exile-island of the Song imagination — and prodigious by any standard (poems at seven, the nine classics at nine, the boy-doctorate at twelve). Then the standard road closed: the examinations rejected him (the records note the examiners' judgment — too wild). The Daoist Culture Centre record adds the sharpest biographical item, and the site registers it as the record gives it: a chivalrous killing, flight to Wuyi — and the turn to the Way that follows. The site's [Zhang Boduan page](/people/zhang-boduan/) holds the southern lineage's earlier turning-point (the burned documents, the exile south); Bai Yuchan is the same genre of biography, one century later, with the violence kept in.
- **Nine years with Chen Nan, and then the break with the lineage's own habit.** The records are specific: meeting Chen Nan (the fourth patriarch), nine years of service, the transmission at Luofu in 1212 — and then the act that makes him the school's actual founder: taking disciples *openly* (1217), founding a teaching seat, and ending the southern lineage's one-to-one transmission. The site's [organizer arc](/people/qiu-chuji/) holds the comparison in place: where Qiu Chuji scaled the northern teaching through the Mongol court's patronage, Bai Yuchan scaled the southern teaching through sheer open teaching — "scholars came from all directions like hairs on an ox." The site registers both as the same historical move (from transmission to institution) done by opposite means.
- **The mad-saint image, registered as strategy.** The records keep the portrait (barefoot, blue scarf, "now running wild, now sitting still, now sleeping all day, now standing alone through the night, now weeping, now laughing — like one deranged") — and the site files it beside the archive's [anti-display line](/experiences/notes/exp-021-prostrations-channel-circuit/): this is the inverse of display. Where the archive's teacher warns against showing attainment, the tradition's records *preserve the showing* of madness — and the site's [Wang Chongyang page](/people/wang-chongyang/) holds the same pattern a generation earlier (the living-dead tomb, the self-named "madman Wang"). The site registers the pattern as the tradition's own publicity idiom, without endorsing its metaphysics.
- **The thunder that obeys the heart.** The Daoist Culture Centre record preserves the doctrine that makes Bai Yuchan matter to the site's [claim discipline](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/): the thunder-rites work *only as the operator's inner work does* — 法是心之臣，心是法之主 (the method is the heart's minister; the heart is the method's lord) — and the spirits summoned are "the operator's own essence and breath, transformed." That is the tradition's own rationalization of its most supernatural machinery: efficacy is moved entirely inside the practitioner. The site's [faith question](/experiences/questions/does-practice-need-faith/) holds the modern form of the same boundary — describe the practice honestly, claim nothing the practice cannot show.
- **The sequence registered, not adjudicated.** The records split on the nature-mind/life-body sequence (the site's [Zhang Boduan page](/people/zhang-boduan/) holds the southern founder's 先命后性, body first; the Daoist Culture Centre records Bai Yuchan as 先性后命, nature first) — and the site keeps both without deciding, per its [dual-registration rule](/people/wang-chongyang/): the southern lineage itself contains both orderings, and the site's practice pages take no side.
- **Why he belongs here.** He closes the site's [southern arc](/people/zhang-boduan/): Zhang Boduan wrote the text, Bai Yuchan built the school — and he is also the site's standing counterweight to the monk-sage type: the poet, the calligrapher, the man whose collected works fill volumes and whose poem sits in the children's anthology (白云黄鹤道人家 — white clouds, yellow crane, the Daoist home), while the [essence-breath-spirit](/glossary/jing-qi-shen/) teaching he transmitted runs the site's own glossary. The book-learning and the sitting belong to the same man — which is, for this site, the whole point.
