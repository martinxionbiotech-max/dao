## The man

- **The clerk who burned the files.** The record is agreed on the frame: a Northern Song official-scholar (jinshi degree, prefectural clerk, exiled for the offense the tradition calls 火烧文书 — burning his office's documents in a moment of renunciation) who turned, in mid-life, to the Way he had studied all along. His own preface (quoted verbatim in the verification) lists the studies — three teachings' scriptures, law, mathematics, medicine, divination, formations, astronomy, geography, the arts of death and life — 靡不留心详究: nothing he did not study with care. The catalog of a Confucian polymath, in service of an alchemist.
- **1069, Chengdu.** The tradition's hinge-year: serving under the official Lu Shen, he was in Chengdu in the jiyou year and — in his own preface's account — his sincerity drew a "perfected person" who transmitted 金丹药物火候之诀, the golden-elixir method of drug and fire-timing. The later tradition identifies the transmitter as Liu Haichan; the site registers the identification as the tradition's own, and the transmission as his own claim: what is historically his is the *text* the transmission produced.
- **The Wuzhen pian: alchemy as verse.** Completed in 1075, the book states the whole method in poetry — 16 regulated verses, 64 quatrains, 12 Xijiangyue lyrics, and more — and states its doctrine in four words that became the Southern lineage's charter: 性命双修，先命后性 — nature (性) and life (命) both cultivated, *life first*. The body is the furnace (以身为鼎炉), essence and qi the drug, spirit the fire — the [three-treasures vocabulary](/glossary/jing-qi-shen/) the site already holds, organized into a program. Its famous sibling is the [Zhouyi cantong qi](/texts/); its closing sections absorb Chan — the three-teachings confluence the site has tracked from [Lu Xiujing](/people/lu-xiujing/) through [Zhiyi](/people/zhiyi/).
- **Three transmissions, three disasters — and the book's honest sequel.** The postface records 三传非人，三遭祸患: three transmissions to the wrong men, three disasters. The Hongdao study (registered in verification) sorts the chronology the tradition itself muddied: the pre-book three (his active search in Qin-Long) and the post-book three (students who came and whom he could not bear to refuse) are *different episodes* — the book itself is the divider. The site holds this as the alchemical tradition's own teaching-ethics document: transmission is the danger point, not the method.
- **The lineage he did not found.** He transmitted to Shi Tai; the line runs Shi Tai → Xue Daoguang → Chen Nan → Bai Yuchan, who built the actual Southern school (南宗) — Zhang Boduan is its 初祖 (first ancestor) by title, not by organization: in his lifetime he was a poet-teacher with three bad students. The Yuan enfeoffed him 紫阳真人; the Qing Yongzheng emperor, centuries later, made him Daci Yuantong Chanxian Ziyang zhenren and ordered a temple built at his old Tiantai site — the tradition's own demonstration that canonization is posthumous construction, registered as such.

## Why he belongs in this knowledge base

Because he is the site's clearest *body-first* counterweight. The [Zuowang](/practices/zuowang/), [Shouyi](/practices/shouyi/), and [Neiguan](/practices/cunsi/) lineages the site has documented run through the mind to the body; Zhang Boduan's charter — 先命后性 — runs through the body to the mind, with the body as furnace, drug, and fire. The site's [experience archive](/experiences/) documents contemporary teachers who say the same thing in Buddhist language (the body is the key; the mind-methods come after) — the phrasing converges; the lineages do not, and the site keeps them apart. He is also the tradition's own proof that its deepest technical literature can be *beautiful*: the Wuzhen pian is the site's answer to readers who assume alchemical writing must be opaque — the tradition's most systematic text is also its most composed.
