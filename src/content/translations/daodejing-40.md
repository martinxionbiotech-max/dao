## Chinese text

反者道之动，弱者道之用。天下万物生于有，有生于无。

## Pinyin

Fǎn zhě dào zhī dòng, ruò zhě dào zhī yòng. Tiānxià wànwù shēng yú yǒu, yǒu shēng yú wú.

## Literal rendering

Returning is the Way's motion; the soft is the Way's use. All things under heaven are born from being; being is born from nothing.

## Term-by-term notes

- **The book's shortest chapter, at the book's center.** Two sentences, twenty-one characters — and the tradition places it at the hinge between the Dao and De halves of the received book. The site notes the placement without pressing it: whatever the chapter-division's history, this chapter states the engine that runs every inversion the book has made — the [ch. 36 reversals](/translations/daodejing-36/) (gather/spread, weaken/strengthen) are this chapter's sentence applied to politics; the [ch. 22 turns](/translations/daodejing-22/) are it applied to conduct; the [ch. 11 emptiness](/translations/daodejing-11/) is its generation-formula applied to artifacts.
- **反 — the word with two senses.** The academic record (registered in verification, citing Qian Zhongshu) holds the two readings: 反 as *reversal* (the opposite direction) and 反 as *return* (coming back). The book's own ch. 25 chain — 大曰逝，逝曰远，远曰反 (great, then flowing away; flowing away, then far; far, then returning) — the site's [ch. 25 page](/translations/daodejing-25/) — supplies the gloss: the word means *both at once*, the motion that reaches the limit and turns back. Every extreme in the book is a point of departure for its opposite — the chapter is the book's statement of that law, and the site holds it beside the [reversal chapter](/translations/daodejing-36/) as law-and-case.
- **弱 — the soft as the Way's working.** 弱者道之用: the chapter's second half-line names the *use* of the Way as the weak, the yielding. The word is the book's standing vocabulary — the [ch. 36](/translations/daodejing-36/) 柔弱胜刚强, the infant-and-stiff-corpse of ch. 76 — and here it is promoted to definition: not "the Way is weak" but "the Way *works as* the weak." The site's [non-action](/concepts/wuwei/) pages hold the practice consequence: the soft is the only mode that returns, because the hard has nowhere to go but break.
- **The generation formula.** 天下万物生于有，有生于无 — things from being, being from nothing: the chapter's cosmology in one line, and the book's own summary of the [ch. 2 pair](/translations/daodejing-02/) and the [ch. 11 artifact argument](/translations/daodejing-11/). The site registers the formula's standing place in the later tradition — the inner-alchemical reading took 无 as the pre-cosmic root of the [inner work](/people/zhang-boduan/) — and holds the text-level claim modest: the chapter states a direction of dependence (being leans on nothing), not a mechanism.
- **The note on the word's later life.** 反者道之动 entered the tradition's proverb stock as 物极必反 (things at their extreme reverse) — the popularized form the chapter itself never uses, and which trims the word to its reversal sense only. The site keeps both senses in the translation ("returning," glossed) per its [term discipline](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/): the law the chapter states is that every extreme is a turning — reversal *is* the return.
