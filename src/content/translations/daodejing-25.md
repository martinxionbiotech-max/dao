## Chinese text

有物混成，先天地生。寂兮寥兮，独立而不改，周行而不殆，可以为天下母。吾不知其名，字之曰道，强为之名曰大。大曰逝，逝曰远，远曰反。故道大，天大，地大，王亦大。域中有四大，而王居其一焉。人法地，地法天，天法道，道法自然。

## Pinyin

Yǒu wù hùn chéng, xiān tiāndì shēng. Jì xī liáo xī, dúlì ér bù gǎi, zhōu xíng ér bù dài, kěyǐ wéi tiānxià mǔ. Wú bùzhī qí míng, zì zhī yuē Dào, qiáng wéi zhī míng yuē dà. Dà yuē shì, shì yuē yuǎn, yuǎn yuē fǎn. Gù dào dà, tiān dà, dì dà, wáng yì dà. Yù zhōng yǒu sì dà, ér wáng jū qí yī yān. Rén fǎ dì, dì fǎ tiān, tiān fǎ dào, dào fǎ zìrán.

## Literal rendering

There is a thing formed in the mingling, born before heaven and earth. Silent and empty, it stands alone and does not alter, goes round and round and does not tire — it can be the mother of all under heaven. I do not know its name; I style it the Dao; were I forced to name it, I would call it Great. Great means passing onward; passing onward means going far; going far means turning back. So the Dao is great, heaven is great, earth is great, and the king is also great. Within the realm there are four greats, and the king holds one of them. The human follows the earth; the earth follows heaven; heaven follows the Dao; **the Dao follows what is so of itself.**

## Term-by-term notes

- **混成 (hùn chéng, "formed in the mingling"):** the thing *before* the distinctions — the same pre-differentiated hum as the [drumming-basin](/translations/drumming-basin-passage/) passage's 芒芴 and the [qiwulun](/translations/qiwulun-wu-sang-wo/) blur. The Dao is not a god who creates; it is the *mingled given* out of which heaven and earth arise.
- **寂兮寥兮 (silent and empty):** the two adjectives that will become the vocabulary of sitting: the [jing](/concepts/jing/) page's stillness and the [xu](/concepts/xu/) page's emptiness both trace back to this pair. The Dao's quiet is not absence but *standing alone* — 独立 — the tradition's word for what the practice tries to become.
- **大曰逝，逝曰远，远曰反 (Great means passing; passing means far; far means turning back):** the four-name ladder, and the most contested rung is the last: 反 — "return." The [DDJ 16 translation](/translations/daodejing-16/) works the same verb (归根, returning to the root): the Dao's farthest reach *turns back*, and the sitting traditions inherit the geometry — the mind sent out to the edges, then gathered home.
- **王亦大 / 人亦大 (the king / the human is also great):** the recensions split here — the Heshang Gong text reads 王 (king), many others read 人 (human). The page keeps both, per the [translation policy](/blog/translation-policy/): the "four greats" are either ruler-inclusive (king, heaven, earth, Dao) or species-inclusive (human, heaven, earth, Dao) — and either way, the point is that *someone here* belongs in the list with the Dao.
- **人法地，地法天，天法道，道法自然:** the four-rung ladder, and the line this site's [ziran concept page](/concepts/ziran/) exists to protect. The verb 法 (follow, take as the standard) repeats four times, and the final rung does not say "the Dao follows the Dao" — it says the Dao follows **自然**, "what is so of itself." Two readings of the chain are both live in scholarship, and the page keeps both: (a) *ziran* as the ultimate standard the Dao itself answers to — the chain ends in a value, not a thing; (b) *ziran* as the Dao's own manner — the chain ends where it began, and the last line means "the Dao is its own standard." Either way, the line is the tradition's anti-foundation: nothing stands *under* the Dao, and nothing needs to.
- **强为之名 (forced to name it):** the book's standing apology for every word in it — including this translation. Naming is violence done politely; the site's [translation policy](/blog/translation-policy/) takes the apology seriously: terms stay in pinyin, glosses stay glosses.

## Where this passage sits

Chapter 25 is the book's cosmology in one page — and, with [ch. 16](/translations/daodejing-16/), the other half of the site's DDJ translation pair: ch. 16 gives the practice (empty to the utmost, guard stillness), ch. 25 gives the world the practice points at (the self-so order). Together they are the site's shortest answer to what the sitting tradition is *about*.
