## Chinese text

上善若水。水善利万物而不争，处众人之所恶，故几于道。居善地，心善渊，与善仁，言善信，正善治，事善能，动善时。夫唯不争，故无尤。

## Pinyin

Shàng shàn ruò shuǐ. Shuǐ shàn lì wàn wù ér bù zhēng, chǔ zhòng rén zhī suǒ wù, gù jī yú dào. Jū shàn dì, xīn shàn yuān, yǔ shàn rén, yán shàn xìn, zhèng shàn zhì, shì shàn néng, dòng shàn shí. Fú wéi bù zhēng, gù wú yóu.

## Literal rendering

The highest good is like water. Water's goodness benefits the ten thousand things and does not contend; it settles in the place the crowd dislikes, and so is near the Way. In dwelling, good at the low place; in mind, good at the deep pool; in giving, good at kindness; in words, good at trust; in governing, good at order; in work, good at ability; in moving, good at timing. Only because it does not contend, nothing finds fault with it.

## Term-by-term notes

- **The near-Way.** 几于道 — *nearly* the Way. The book's whole discipline is in that 几 (nearly): water is the closest image, never the thing itself. The [creator question](/questions/does-daoism-believe-in-a-creator/) holds the family of near-images — the clod, the loom, the steward — and water is their liquid member: the world's own process, shown as an element.
- **The two halves of the reason.** 利万物 (benefiting all things) without 争 (contending), and 处众人之所恶 (dwelling where the crowd dislikes) — the *giving* and the *descending*. The chapter's water is not a metaphor for humility but a statement about how benefit actually moves: it flows to the low place *because* it benefits — the two clauses are one motion, and the site's [wuwei concept](/concepts/wuwei/) reads them as the book's definition of non-doing: the force that works by seeking no victory.
- **The seven skills.** 居善地，心善渊，与善仁，言善信，正善治，事善能，动善时 — dwelling, mind, giving, words, governing, work, moving: each given its water-form. The tradition's glosses (Su Zhe, quoted in verification) read them as water's own virtues — 避高趋下 (avoiding the high, seeking the low) for dwelling; 空虚寂寞，深不可测 (empty, still, unfathomable) for the mind's depth. The site flags two: 心善渊 — the [stillness family's](/concepts/jing/) own image, the deep pool that reflects without stirring (the [zhi-shui glossary](/glossary/zhi-shui/) holds its Zhuangzi twin); and 正善治 — the textual variant 政善治 recorded in verification (received 正 vs. one tradition's 政, "governing"), registered without adjudication per the site's [edition discipline](/translations/daodejing-22/).
- **The closing formula.** 夫唯不争，故无尤 — "only because it does not contend, nothing finds fault" — the book's most repeated cadence (ch. 22: 夫唯不争，故天下莫能与之争). 尤 is not punishment but *finding fault*: the uncontending has no enemies because it has no fronts. The [empty boat](/stories/xuzhou-empty-boat/) is this line as a story — the boat no one can be angry at.
- **The water family.** The site's [swimmer](/stories/swimmer-lvliang/) enters the water's way (从水之道), the [fish pages](/stories/fish-happiness/) know its depths, and the [dry-spring fish](/stories/fish-in-the-dry-spring/) miss it — this chapter is the family's charter: the element the book keeps returning to when it needs to show the Way *working*.
