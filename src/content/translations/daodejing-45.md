## Chinese text

大成若缺，其用不弊。大盈若冲，其用不穷。大直若屈，大巧若拙，大辩若讷。躁胜寒，静胜热。清静为天下正。

## Pinyin

Dà chéng ruò quē, qí yòng bù bì. Dà yíng ruò chōng, qí yòng bù qióng. Dà zhí ruò qū, dà qiǎo ruò zhuō, dà biàn ruò nè. Zào shèng hán, jìng shèng rè. Qīngjìng wéi tiānxià zhèng.

## Literal rendering

The great completion seems broken, yet its use never wears out. The great fullness seems empty, yet its use never ends. The great straightness seems bent, the great skill seems clumsy, the great eloquence seems mute. Agitation overcomes cold, stillness overcomes heat. Clear-stillness is the world's true standard.

## Term-by-term notes

- **Five finished things, each in its worn form.** The chapter's inventory is five 大-words — completion, fullness, straightness, skill, eloquence — and each appears in its own *opposite* clothing: 若缺 (seems broken), 若冲 (seems empty), 若屈 (seems bent), 若拙 (seems clumsy), 若讷 (seems mute). The hinge is the verb 若, *seems*: the text does not say the great completion is broken — it says the assessment of the appearance is the error, and the appearance is real. The site's [bell-stand page](/stories/bei-gong-she/) holds the sequence in narrative form (既雕既琢，复归于朴 — carved and polished, then returned to the block): the finished thing, seen finished, looks unfinished.
- **The two "use" clauses and their family.** 其用不弊 / 其用不穷 — its use never wears out, its use never ends — are the chapter's claims about the worn forms, and they belong to the site's use-family: the [ch. 11 page](/translations/daodejing-11/) (有之以为利，无之以为用 — the having is the advantage, the nothing is the use) and the [ch. 40 page](/translations/daodejing-40/) (弱者道之用 — the weak is the Way's use). The worn-out-looking thing is the one whose use *cannot* wear out, because the wear was already there to see; the polished thing's use dies with its polish. The site's [useless gourd](/stories/useless-gourd/) is the same arithmetic in a story: the gourd too big to fill is the gourd whose use never ends.
- **The craftsman's clause.** 大巧若拙 — the great skill seems clumsy — is the book's sentence for the site's whole [craftsman line](/stories/artisan-qing/): the bell-stand carver who works like a man without skill (no wasted motion, nothing to show between stages), the [cook](/stories/cook-ding/) whose knife never needs sharpening, the [wheelwright](/stories/wheelwright-bian/) who cannot put his knack into words. What looks like clumsiness from outside the craft is the absence of *display* — and the site's [bird lesson](/stories/yi-dai-niao/) (the dithering bird that looks unable and is never harmed) is the same sentence in survival form. 大辩若讷 — the great eloquence seems mute — points the other way, at the [five-cart debater](/stories/hui-shi-wu-che/): the book's own rival, eloquent beyond measure, silenced by a fish's happiness.
- **The closing pair, registered with its variant.** 躁胜寒，静胜热 — agitation overcomes cold, stillness overcomes heat — is the chapter's only sentence that is not a 大-pair, and the editions disagree on its order (some print 静胜躁，寒胜热; the site registers both without adjudicating). The received text's logic is the body's: against cold you generate agitation, against heat you keep still — both true, both second-best — and the third line lands the standard: 清静为天下正, clear-stillness is the world's true standard (正 also glossed as 政, government, in the traditional notes: the standard and the governance are one word). The site's [stillness pages](/glossary/wuwei/) hold the family: the chapter's five worn forms and its closing stillness are one doctrine — what looks worn from the outside is, from the inside, exactly what does not wear.
- **The chapter's boundary with ch. 55.** The site's [ch. 55 page](/translations/daodejing-55/) holds the infant's unspent body; this chapter holds the adult's worn body. Together they are the book's two physiologies of the finished thing: the newborn is complete *before* spending, the great completion is complete *after* it — and both are protected by the same clause, registered here as the site's standing boundary: the tradition's claim is about use and wear, not about health, and the [qi page](/glossary/qi/) holds the discipline on what these pages do and do not assert.
