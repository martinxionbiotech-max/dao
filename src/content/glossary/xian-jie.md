## The term

县解 (xuánjiě) — 县, the ancient graph for a suspended head (read and written 悬, to hang); 解, to untie, to release. The term appears exactly twice in the Zhuangzi, both times as the reward of the same formula:

- Over a **death** (ch. 3, [Qin Shi mourning Laozi](/stories/qinshi-mourning/)): 安时而处顺，哀乐不能入也，古者谓是**帝之县解** — "rest in the season and dwell in what follows; sorrow and joy cannot get in — the ancients called this **Heaven's release from the hanging**."
- Over a **sickness** (ch. 6, [Zi Yu's crumpled body](/stories/four-friends/)): 得者时也，失者顺也，安时而处顺，哀乐不能入也，此古之所谓**县解**也 — "what I got, I got in its season; what I lose, I lose in the order of things; rest in the season and dwell in what follows; sorrow and joy cannot get in — this is what the ancients called **release from the hanging**."

## Why the term matters

- **The hanging is the body's bond, not the body.** The image is a person tied head-down — and the release is not from life or from the body, but from the *binding*: what hangs one is attachment's grip, the terror of what comes. The [education encyclopedia's gloss](https://pedia.cloud.edu.tw/), verified on the [Qin Shi page](/stories/qinshi-mourning/), states it: 死生无动于心 — life and death not moving the heart. The site keeps this because it is the term's whole correction: the tradition does not promise release from dying; it promises release from the *punishment* of dying (the ch. 3 passage calls the un-released state 遁天之刑, the punishment of fleeing Heaven).
- **One formula, two scenes.** The word-for-word identity of the formula in ch. 3 and ch. 6 — same clause, same term, one over a death, one over a sickness — is the book's own evidence that 县解 is one thing, not two: the release is from *whatever* comes, because what comes is always "in its season" (时) or "in the order" (顺). The site's two story pages cross-reference by this formula.
- **Its relation to the forgetting-family.** The [zuowang](/concepts/zuowang/) concept is the *method* (sitting in oblivion), the [liang-wang](/glossary/liang-wang/) term the *social form* (forgetting judgments), and 县解 is the *result-word*: what the untying feels like when the body is the rope. The three terms form the site's release-vocabulary, and this page is its anchor.

## Usage notes

The term stays in pinyin with the gloss "release from the hanging"; the graphic note (县 = 悬) is kept because the suspended-head image is the term's engine; both occurrences are cited, per the site's rule that a term with two attested uses gets both.
