## The term

天机 (tiānjī) — 天, heaven, the natural; 机, mechanism — the loom's working part, the trigger, the catch that lets a thing run. The term appears in the Zhuangzi in two of the site's [verified passages](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/), both times naming the same thing:

- The **centipede** (ch. 17): 今予动吾天机，而不知其所以然 — "I move my heaven-mechanism and do not know how it works."
- The **snake** (ch. 17): 夫天机之所动，何可易邪？吾安用足哉？ — "How can what the heaven-mechanism moves be exchanged? What use would I have for feet?"
- And the tradition's own gloss, in Cheng Xuanying's subcommentary on the [penumbra fable](/stories/wang-liang-wen-jing/) (ch. 2): 天机自张，莫知其宰 — "the heaven-mechanism spreads itself; no one knows its driver."

## Why the term matters

- **It is the 自 family's machinery-word.** The [ziran concept page](/concepts/ziran/) holds the family (自然, 自化, 自取) — the book's words for things running *themselves*. 天机 is the family's most mechanical member: the mechanism that runs, whose driver is not known. The centipede's answer is the family's creed in one sentence: it moves, and does not know how — and the snake's is the creed's refusal of envy: what the mechanism moves **cannot be exchanged**, so questions like "why my feet and not yours" have no hold.
- **Not a machine, and not a secret.** The term's two modern corruptions are both documented on this page: (1) machine-agnostic readings that make 天机 sound like a clockwork — the text's own word is 张, *spreads itself*, a loom's self-unfolding, not a mechanism with a maker; and (2) the **idiom drift** that matters most: modern Chinese 天机 survives chiefly in 天机不可泄露 — "heaven's secrets must not be revealed" — where 天机 has become *a secret to be kept*. The Zhuangzi's 天机 is the opposite of a secret: it is the open, self-running order, and the centipede *tells* it plainly. The site flags the drift on every use, per its [idiom-drift discipline](/stories/fish-in-the-dry-spring/).
- **莫知其宰 — no driver known.** Cheng Xuanying's gloss ties the term to the chapter-2 question (若有真宰 — "as if there were a true driver") and answers it negatively: the mechanism spreads itself, and the driver is *not known* — not denied, not affirmed, left unlocatable. The term is the site's anchor for how the tradition handles the question of agency: movement without a locatable mover.

## Usage notes

The term stays in pinyin with the gloss "heaven-mechanism"; the two ch. 17 attestations are always cited together (the site's rule for terms with multiple uses); the modern "secret" drift is flagged wherever the term appears; 张 (spreads itself) is kept against "machine" translations.
