## The term

葆光 (bǎoguāng) — 葆, to shelter and keep hidden; 光, light. The term closes a passage in ch. 2 that builds toward it through five negations — 大道不称 (the great Way is not named), 大辩不言 (the great argument does not speak), 大仁不仁, 大廉不嗛, 大勇不忮 — each displayed form failing its own virtue. Then the passage asks: 孰知不言之辩、不道之道？ — who knows the argument without words, the Way without a way? And answers in two images, one noun and one verb: **天府**, the heavenly storehouse — 注焉而不满，酌焉而不竭，而不知其所由来 — pour into it and it does not fill, ladle out of it and it does not empty, and no one knows where it comes from; and **葆光**, the concealed light — the storehouse's radiance, known precisely by not being shown.

## Why the term matters

- **A depth-image, not a brightness-image.** The tradition's own light-word is built from pouring and ladling — capacity, not brilliance. The site's [light problem page](/problems/seeing-light-in-sitting/) holds the practical distinction this term makes from inside the tradition: practitioners report *seeing* light (imagery in the visual field, registered and bounded there), while the book's 葆光 is a claim about the *unseen* source — 而不知其所由来. The two are not enemies, but they are not the same word, and the site keeps the tradition's word for the hidden one.
- **The dark mirror's family.** The scholarly reading (registered in verification) links 葆光 to the Daodejing's 玄览 — the dark mirror — and 玄德, hidden virtue: the sage does not display an extraordinary brightness. This is the site's [seeing family](/glossary/jian-du/) in its self-effacing register — [虚室生白](/glossary/xushi-sheng-bai/) (the empty room's whiteness), [朝彻](/glossary/jian-du/) (dawn-clearness), and 葆光 (the sheltered light) are three ways of saying one thing: the light that comes of emptying is not for exhibition.
- **The inexhaustible as the standard.** 注焉而不满，酌焉而不竭 is the book's own definition of a depth worth having: something can be poured into endlessly and drawn from endlessly, with no known origin. The site's [water pages](/translations/daodejing-08/) — the near-Way of ch. 8, the [deep pool](/glossary/zhi-shui/) of the still mind — are this image's liquid kin, and the [settling practice](/practices/jingzuo/) aims at nothing else: a depth that gives without showing and receives without filling.
- **Against the flaunted state.** The term's immediate context is the five negations — and the chapter's longer argument against 荣华 (the flourishing display of argument). The site's [EXP-021](/experiences/notes/exp-021-prostrations-channel-circuit/) and [EXP-022](/experiences/notes/exp-022-its-all-theatre/) pages hold the same line in the practice record: the tradition's own people warn against performing attainment. 葆光 is the vocabulary: attainment is a storehouse, not a display — and the display is how it leaks.
- **The drift, small but real.** 葆光 survives in modern Chinese as a learned word for hidden talent or restrained brilliance — the *concealment* survives, but the *source* (the inexhaustible storehouse, 不知其所由来) has thinned into mere modesty. The site registers the drift per its [idiom discipline](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/): the shell of the word survives; the cosmology is carried only in the chapter.
