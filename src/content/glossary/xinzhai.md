**Xinzhai** (心斋, xīnzhāi) — "fasting of the mind," the *Zhuangzi* ch. 4 instruction to listen not with ear or mind but with qi, emptying the mind so the Dao gathers in it: "Emptiness — that is the fasting of the mind."

**Translation note:** *xin* in classical Chinese covers both "heart" and "mind"; "fasting of the mind" is standard, but "heart-mind" is a useful one-time gloss for readers who need the warning. Avoid "mental fast" (sounds like a diet trend).
