## The term

环中 (huánzhōng) — "the center of the ring." The phrase is the pivot passage's second half: the things and their counterparts never find their match (彼是莫得其偶), and *that* is called the hinge of the Way (道枢); and the hinge, once found, comes to rest at the center of the ring (枢始得其环中), and from there answers the endless (以应无穷) — for the endless is on both sides: this is also an endless, that is also an endless (是亦一无穷，非亦一无穷也). Therefore: better to use the light (故曰莫若以明).

## Why the term matters

- **The geometry does the arguing.** The ring is the book's answer to the tournament of yes-and-no without joining it: every this and every that sits on the circumference, and the center belongs to none of them — so it can answer all of them. The site's [tian-jun page](/glossary/tian-jun/) holds the wheel (the turning balance, not the scale); 环中 is the wheel's *address* — where the pivot lives. The two terms are one passage's two halves, kept separate on the site because the tradition uses them for different jobs: 天钧 names the balance, 环中 names the stance.
- **The stance, and the response it makes possible.** From the center, the endless round can be *answered* — 以应无穷 — and the book's own exemplar of answering is the [mirror heart](/translations/yongxin-ruo-jing-passage/) (ch. 7: responds and stores nothing, 应而不藏) and the [still water](/glossary/zhi-shui/) (ch. 5: no one mirrors themselves in running water). The site's [empty-boat page](/stories/xuzhou-empty-boat/) is the same geometry in a story: the collision happens, and only the *fuel* changes — the empty boat does not dodge; it is simply not where the anger is. The ring's center is not a shelter from events; it is the position from which events stop being sides.
- **What the center is not.** The site holds two misreadings at arm's length, per its [claim discipline](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/): 环中 is not *detachment* (the passage does not stop answering — it answers the endless, and the [shaman page](/stories/huzi-jixian/) shows the book's master responding to each visitor differently, four times), and it is not *relativism* (莫若以明 — better to use the light — is the chapter's own warning that the center is a source of light, not a shrug). The [qiwu page](/glossary/qiwu/) holds the fuller argument: the leveling ends the tournament; it does not award it to everyone.
- **The hinge first, the center second.** 道枢 comes before 环中 — the hinge of the Way is what finds the center. The order is the doctrine: the position is not chosen by will but *arrived at* by the hinge — the same order as the site's [wu-sang-wo page](/glossary/wu-sang-wo/) (the me is lost first; the pivot follows). For the site's practice pages the term's work is quiet and constant: the [sitting protocol](/tools/sitting-protocol/) asks for nothing more than the hinge's beginning — the daily return to the center, not the perfect stance.
- **The recommendation.** "The center of the ring" — kept as a term with its geometry intact, because every softening of it ("the middle way," "centeredness") loses the ring. The image is the argument: only a circle has a center that belongs to no side.
