**Qi** (气, qì) — the classical Chinese term for the subtle substrate between body and mind: vapor, breath, vitality. In contemplative texts it names the level "empty and awaiting things" where the Dao gathers (*Zhuangzi* ch. 4), and the vital element kept from dissipating in shouyi practice.

**Translation note:** no single English word works. "Energy" suggests physics and invites pseudoscientific readings; "breath" covers only one register; "vital force" carries vitalist baggage. Best practice (followed on this site): keep *qi* romanized, gloss once ("vital breath/energy"), and let context specify. Never translate as "God," "spirit," or "the Force."
