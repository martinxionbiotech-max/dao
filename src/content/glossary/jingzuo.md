**Jingzuo** (静坐, jìngzuò) — "quiet sitting," the seated contemplative practice of the Song–Ming Neo-Confucian tradition: sitting to collect and stabilize the mind for study and moral conduct, associated with Zhu Xi's recorded formula "half a day quiet sitting, half a day reading."

**Translation note:** a comparatively easy term — "quiet sitting" is literal and idiomatic. Avoid "meditation" alone (it obscures the Confucian rationale); avoid "still sitting" (loses the everyday sense of *jing* as quiet/calm).
