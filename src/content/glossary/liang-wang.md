## The term

两忘 (liǎngwàng) — 两, the pair, the two; 忘, to forget, let go, put aside. The Zhuangzi uses it once, in the [Dazongshi](/stories/fish-in-the-dry-spring/) chapter, as the second of two parallel lines:

"Rather than praise Yao and condemn Jie, better to **forget both** and transform along the Way."
(与其誉尧而非桀也，不如两忘而化其道。)

The first line is the fish: when the spring dries up, the fish on the dry ground keep each other damp with their spit — "rather than that, better to forget each other in the rivers and lakes." The second line moves the same structure from the *mutual* register (相忘, the fish forgetting each other) to the *judging* register (两忘, forgetting both sides of the verdict).

## Why the term matters

- **The pair is the object, not the sides.** The term does not say "judge more fairly between Yao and Jie" or "find the middle." It dissolves the *pairing* itself — the stance that generates praise-and-blame. The fish line shows what the pair actually is: the [spit of the stranded](/stories/fish-in-the-dry-spring/), a survival mechanism of the dried-out, not a healthy function of the mind. 两忘 is the tradition's one-word refusal of the judging stance — the same refusal the [mirror passage](/translations/yongxin-ruo-jing-passage/) encodes as 不将不迎, no escorting out and no welcoming in: praise is an escort, blame a refusal, and the mirror does neither.
- **化其道 — the forgetting is not an end.** The line does not stop at "forget both." It says forget both *and transform along the Way* — 化, the word for flowing change, the same verb in the fish passage's own water. Forgetting clears the channel; what then flows is not a new opinion but the Way's own movement. The site's [zuowang](/concepts/zuowang/) concept page is this structure's full development: the forgetting is the subtraction, and the subtraction's product is never named in advance.
- **It completes the forgetting family's social member.** [Zuowang](/concepts/zuowang/) forgets the self; [the ferryman](/stories/boatman/) forgets the water; 两忘 forgets the verdicts. The one the [three-languages](/blog/three-languages/) discipline most needs is this one: the editorial rule that keeps traditional claims, scientific findings, and practitioner reports *separate but unranked* is a 两忘 discipline — the site refuses to run a league table among its own registers.

## Usage notes

The term appears later in Buddhist-influenced usage (两忘 as a Chan-flavored complementarity), but the site keeps the Dazongshi usage as primary, in pinyin with the gloss "forgetting both," per the [translation policy](/blog/translation-policy/).
