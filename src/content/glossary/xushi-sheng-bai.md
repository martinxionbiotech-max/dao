## The term

虚室生白 (xūshì shēng bái) — the empty room gives birth to brightness. The term comes from the book's [heart-fasting passage](/concepts/xinzhai/) (ch. 4), in the climax the site holds in full:

瞻彼阕者，虚室生白，吉祥止止 — "Look at that opening: the empty room gives birth to brightness; the auspicious comes to rest, rests."

The sentence before it is the passage's method (气也者，虚而待物者也。唯道集虚。虚者，心斋也 — "the qi is empty and awaits things; only the Way gathers in the empty; the empty is the fasting of the mind"), and the sentence after it is the passage's warning (夫且不止，是之谓坐驰 — "if it does not come to rest, this is called sitting-while-galloping," the site's [zuochi page](/glossary/zuochi/)).

## Why the term matters

- **The room is the heart.** The passage's room (室) is the heart's room — the metaphor the [xinzhai page](/concepts/xinzhai/) documents in full: stop the ears at hearing, stop the mind at matching, and what remains is the empty room. The term is the tradition's answer to the question the site's [practice pages](/practices/zuowang/) constantly meet — *what, if anything, is found in the emptiness?* — and the answer is a state-word, not a thing-word: 白, brightness — not an object seen, but the room itself lit. The site keeps the metaphor's grammar strictly: the light is *born from the emptiness*, not placed in it.
- **吉祥止止 — the doubled resting.** The clause's grammar is famous and the tradition reads it as the [stillness family's](/translations/daodejing-16/) consummation: the auspicious (吉祥) comes to rest, and its rest is itself doubled (止止) — "rests, and in resting, rests." The site registers the doubling without choosing among the traditional readings (auspicious-resting / stop-and-it-stops), per the [disagreement rule](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/): what all readings share is the passage's claim that the good is not *sought* but *arrives and stays* — the [wuwei family's](/concepts/wuwei/) grammar applied to fortune itself.
- **The boundary the site enforces.** The term's afterlife in later internal-alchemy and modern 修行 talk turns 虚室生白 into a *vision claim* — "seeing white light in the room." The site's [seeing-light page](/problems/seeing-light-in-sitting/) enforces the boundary: the source-text term is a state-metaphor for the emptied heart; the later light-practice readings belong to later lineages and are not adopted; and the two must not be merged into a license for chasing lights. The [translation policy](/blog/translation-policy/) holds the same line: the term stays in pinyin, glossed as "the empty room gives birth to brightness."
- **The pair with 坐驰.** The passage defines its own opposite in the next breath — 坐驰, sitting while galloping — and the site's [zuochi glossary](/glossary/zuochi/) already holds it. The two terms are one sentence apart and the site keeps them as a pair: the empty room gives birth to brightness; the un-emptied room gallops while sitting. The [drowsiness-and-stillness pattern page](/patterns/drowsiness-vs-stillness/) is the same pair in the site's experience record.

## Usage notes

The term carries its passage's full context whenever cited (the fasting method before it, the galloping warning after it); it is never used to license vision-practices; the 止止 doubling is registered with its readings unadjudicated; cross-linked always with [xinzhai](/concepts/xinzhai/), [xu](/concepts/xu/), and [zuochi](/glossary/zuochi/).
