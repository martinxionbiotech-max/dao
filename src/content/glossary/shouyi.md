**Shouyi** (守一, shǒuyī) — "guarding the One," the early Daoist seated practice of holding attention on the unity of essence (jing), qi, and spirit (shen), rooted in *Daodejing* ch. 10 ("carrying the soul and embracing the One") and systematized in the Han *Taiping Jing* with a concrete chamber protocol.

**Translation note:** "guarding" preserves the active-holding sense (*shou* also means "to keep watch"); "maintaining Oneness" is acceptable but loses the custodial nuance. Capitalize "One" to signal the technical sense of the unified principle.
