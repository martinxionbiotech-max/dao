## The term

机心 (jīxīn) — 机, machine, mechanism, device (the same character as the well-sweep's lever); 心, the heart-mind. The term is coined in the [gardener's refusal](/stories/bao-weng-guan-qi/) (ch. 12), in the book's three-link chain:

吾闻之吾师：有机械者必有机事，有机事者必有机心。机心存于胸中，则纯白不备；纯白不备，则神生不定；神生不定者，道之所不载也。

"I have heard from my teacher: where there are machines, there are machine-dealings; where there are machine-dealings, there is a machine-mind. When a machine-mind lodges in the chest, the pure white is not whole; when the pure white is not whole, the spirit's life is unsettled; and where the spirit's life is unsettled, the Way will not carry it."

## Why the term matters

- **The earliest tool-and-mind argument.** The chain is causal and specific: the machine (机械) does not merely save labor; it *trains* its user — first in its manner of doing (机事, machine-dealings: the lever's rhythm of little-spent, much-gained), then in the heart itself (机心, the machine-mind). The site keeps the chain's grammar because the whole point is that 机心 is not a sin but a *sediment*: the mind becomes what its tools repeatedly do. Two and a half millennia before the argument needed a smartphone to be obvious, the passage had already stated it, with the gardener's coda the site quotes in full on the [story page](/stories/bao-weng-guan-qi/): 吾非不知，羞而不为也 — the refusal is informed, not ignorant.
- **纯白 — the white the machine unmakes.** The chain's damage clause is the site's other [state-term](/glossary/xushi-sheng-bai/) in negative: 纯白不备, the pure white not whole. The [xushi-sheng-bai page](/glossary/xushi-sheng-bai/) holds the white's positive home — 虚室生白, the empty room gives birth to brightness — and the two pages are one claim in two directions: the emptied room *births* the white; the machined chest *loses* it. The [xinzhai concept](/concepts/xinzhai/) is the practice that removes what the machine deposits: the fasting of the mind empties exactly this sediment.
- **神生不定 — the unsettled spirit.** The chain's second damage is the term the [zuochi glossary](/glossary/zuochi/) names in its sitting form: 坐驰, sitting while galloping — the spirit that cannot rest because its life has been unsettled. The passage runs the two together, and the site keeps them together: the machine-mind is 坐驰's *cause*, the empty room its *cure*, and the [zuowang concept](/concepts/zuowang/) is the practice's name for the whole cycle.
- **The word's afterlife, narrowed.** 机心 survives in modern Chinese, but the site flags the drift: the modern word means *scheming, calculating mind* — the chain's third link kept, its first two dropped. The passage's causal argument (machine → machine-mind → unsettled spirit → the Way will not carry it) shrank to a character trait. The word kept its warning tone and lost its argument — the same narrowing the site records for [tian-ji](/glossary/tian-ji/) and the reverse of the intact cases (涸辙之鲋, 蜗角之争).

## Usage notes

The term is always introduced through the full three-link chain, because the chain *is* the definition; it is never used to condemn tools as such — the gardener's refusal is recorded as informed, and the site's own position is the [story page's](/stories/bao-weng-guan-qi/) closing line: treat the machine as a tool, watched; cross-linked with [xushi-sheng-bai](/glossary/xushi-sheng-bai/) (the white), [zuochi](/glossary/zuochi/) (the unsettled spirit), and [xinzhai](/concepts/xinzhai/) (the emptying).
