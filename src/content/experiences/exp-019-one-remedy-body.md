## Why this record matters

This is the archive's record of the teacher's **one-remedy doctrine for the body** — the episode where breath practice becomes the master key that opens every door (teeth, nose, gut, weight, ulcers), and where the archive's health-claims discipline is most needed. It is kept for three reasons: it documents the series' answer to the most common question a sitting practice faces ("my body is misbehaving — is it the practice, and what fixes it?"); it preserves the teacher's breath-first program in full, including the one hard quantitative line (a resting rate near 10–12 per minute before sitting resumes) and the one capped line (300 prostrations for a damaged gut); and it is the archive's clearest instance of the **claims the site records but does not carry** — the anti-Western-medicine warning, the karmic rhinitis, the nerve-point physiology — each registered in the teacher's own terms and each met with the archive's standing refusal to let a meditation column replace medical care.
