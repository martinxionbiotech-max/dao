## Why this record matters

This is the archive's record of the series' **boundary episode** — the one where the teacher's refusal to answer and the teacher's correction of folk misreadings appear together. It is kept for three reasons: the skepticism exchange (a questioner's three-reason materialist case met with "you are not a Buddhist, believe whatever you like" and the theatre doctrine) documents the series' attitude toward questioning at its most revealing, and the site registers it without adopting it; the deep-concentration fear (can one sit to death? can one control the exit?) supplies the [breath-stopping-anxiety page](/problems/breath-stopping-anxiety/) with its clearest traditional-world counterpart — the reassurance that deep concentration is clear, not blank, and that one gets up when it is time; and the yoga answer preserves the episode's most useful correction for practitioners: **the exhaustion-of-outflows is not the stopping of menstruation** — a folk conflation the teacher himself dismantles, which the site records as the series' epistemic hygiene at work, alongside its ceiling claim (yoga, complete as it is, reaches the second dhyāna and works on the body, never surpassing it).

## The record (paraphrased, anonymized)

- **The reports:** a three-reason materialist case against the Buddha's indestructible body; the retreat question; the fear of dying in deep concentration; the yoga-and-menstruation question.
- **The answers, in their own terms:** doubt the sutras if you like — you are not a Buddhist; Kāśyapa's body will not decay; the Buddha turned eight consciousnesses into six and could manifest flesh — his asking Ānanda three times was decided in advance, it is all theatre staged for posterity, and such questions are useless for liberation; we use the five faculties every moment, so sitting must be daily or attachment regresses; the fourth dhyāna was covered earlier; in deep concentration one is clearly aware of the outside, gets up when it is time, and will not die; yoga is older than Buddhism with a complete system, works from the breath, reaches the second dhyāna by the double lotus, works on the body and cannot surpass it — and the exhaustion-of-outflows is the Buddha's last superpower, not the stopping of menstruation.
- **The three layers, kept apart:** the reports; the teacher's framework (the consciousness doctrine, the theatre, the daily-sitting rule, the ceiling claim); and the alternative reading — the metaphysics recorded without endorsement, the daily-sitting rule as a practice claim, the deep-concentration reassurance kept as fear-guidance, and the menstruation correction kept as the episode's most useful line.
