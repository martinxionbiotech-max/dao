## Why this record matters

This is the archive's record of **the hard line, in full**. The [posture question](/experiences/questions/must-i-sit-cross-legged/) already cites, and declines, the claim that "single-lotus doesn't count" — and this record documents the complete system behind that claim, from one teacher in one public Q&A episode: the closed qi-circuit, the five sense-leaks, the zuowang stage, the two-hour single-lotus limit, the electric jolts, and the diagnosis of the composite case. The site keeps the record for three reasons: it is a practitioner-community document of a *living* posture doctrine; its severity (and its internal coherence) is exactly why the site must have its own, independent, source-anchored posture policy; and its two points of convergence with modern practice — alternating the legs, and gradual loading — deserve to be kept even while the doctrine is not adopted.

## The record (paraphrased, anonymized)

- **The reports:** a single-lotus sitter whose method was following the breath into fineness and softness, occasionally to the disappearance of perception, who began to feel sudden electric-shock jolts through the head; the standing questions (can one be liberated without sitting? without wisdom? — "absolutely not"); and the composite case the teacher assembled: single-lotus, no breathing practice, dantian observation — clouded awareness, fading attention and memory, sleep that does not restore.
- **The teacher's system, in its own terms:** double-lotus alone opens the channels — single-lotus is mere quiet sitting; the legs lock the qi into a closed circuit while the eyes, ears, tongue, fingers, and feet are the leaks to be sealed (close the eyes, sit quiet, press the tongue up, hold the samādhi mudrā, lock the legs); the circuit opened, the whole body reaches absolute health; in sitting, the state of forgetting the body's existence arrives — *zuowang* — and that is the gate to the desire-realm dhyānas; single-lotus qi cannot circulate and pools at head and chest until it bursts an aperture and leaks, nearly unsealable — half an hour double-lotus outdoes two hours single, and two hours is the single-lotus limit; alternate the legs even in double-lotus; only two mind-methods — observe the dantian or recite the Buddha-name, never anything above the diaphragm; the electric jolts are pooled qi stimulating nerves at the joints; the composite case has damaged brain cells — stop sitting, build abdominal breathing in a month, then double-lotus both sides and drop the dantian observation; whether you believe in Buddha or the Dao, this whole process is necessary.
- **The three layers, kept apart:** the practitioner reports (the jolts, the fading, the questions), the traditional framework (the closed circuit, the leaks, the stage named *zuowang*), and the alternative reading (hypnic-jerk family, the tradition's physiology as doctrine not anatomy, the site's non-adoption and its own posture policy, the two convergences kept). The site records the system without endorsing it — the same separation the [three-languages](/blog/three-languages/) discipline requires everywhere.
