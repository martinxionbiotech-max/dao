## Why this record matters

This is the site's clearest archived case of the **dissolution-vs-blank-staring question** — the exact boundary the [zuochi](/glossary/zuochi/) glossary and the [drowsiness-vs-stillness](/experiences/patterns/drowsiness-vs-stillness/) pattern exist to mark. A practitioner reported what felt like a genuine merger with emptiness; the teacher responded with the tradition's sharpest diagnostic word (发呆, "blank staring") and the instruction not to chase states. Both the experience and the diagnosis are kept here, in three layers, without this site adjudicating between them.

## The record (paraphrased, anonymized)

- **The method reported:** two phases — first, mantra plus visualization with deliberate effort; second, a switch to an "inner layer of awareness" repeating sutra phrases on emptiness, during which the practitioner felt self-awareness fading with the phrases, "becoming empty," merging with the void, with breath noticeably shorter and lighter.
- **The surrounding circumstance:** a painful personal event (a relationship ending) was producing difficulty breathing and weakness, and intrusive distress that interrupted practice — but not during the emptiness phase, where "all afflictions dissolved"; the distress returned outside it.
- **The teacher's response, in its own terms:** the felt dissolution is blank staring, not absorption; before the body's groundwork is complete, practice "only counts as sitting, not meditation"; practice breath-sitting honestly, rise when unable to continue, and do not chase absorption states — elementary absorption takes two to three years of consistent work.

## The boundary this site keeps

The traditional register (channels, absorption stages, the state taxonomy) is reported as the teacher's framework, not adopted as this site's. The alternative reading — a pleasant low-arousal state with reduced self-referential processing, and state-dependent relief from distress rather than resolved emotion — is offered as a *reading*, not a verdict. The [three-languages](/blog/three-languages/) discipline applies in full: practitioner report, traditional diagnosis, and physiological possibility are three separate statements about the same evening, and the record's value is precisely that they disagree.
