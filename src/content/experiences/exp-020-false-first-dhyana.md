## Why this record matters

This is the archive's record of the **false-alarm correction** — the episode where the teacher's attainment-map (channels, heat, desire, the dhyāna ladder) is applied to a real practitioner's ecstatic report, and the report is demoted, gently, to stillness. It is kept for three reasons: it documents the series' most useful single correction for practitioners (warmth, lights, and repeated entries do not equal dhyāna — they equal shamatha, and that is normal); it preserves the episode's two refusals — the three-times-repeated "you tell me first" on the gates and the ruling that the gates are a Chinese accretion, not a sutra standard — which, with [EXP-016](/experiences/notes/exp-016-nine-dhyanas-map/)'s "I have not achieved it myself," form the series' honesty file; and it supplies the [seeing-light page](/problems/seeing-light-in-sitting/) with its clearest traditional-world counterpart: the glowing-eyed Buddha image, ignored and survived.

## The record (paraphrased, anonymized)

- **The reports:** a night of warmth, four entries, entries while lying down, and a Buddha image with glowing eyes just before entry; a body-shudder question; a request for experience over doctrine.
- **The correction, in its own terms:** not first dhyāna — channels unopened, heat undropped, desire unextinguished; what was reached is shamatha; ignore the images, fear nothing (even demons can't reach you yet); the shudder is a joke; seven years of meat-abstinence, then the three-flowers and absolute health, then concentration can drop body-sensation — ten years of work; meditation and prajñā are one; and the gates — "you tell me first," three times, then the ruling: not the Buddha's teaching, a Chinese accretion, unmappable to attainment standards, with the forced mapping (first gate = first dhyāna, second = fourth, life-death = arhatship) offered and the riddle itself refused.
- **The three layers, kept apart:** the reports, the teacher's map, and the alternative reading — the shamatha correction cross-linked to the site's [warmth](/experiences/questions/warmth-tingling-when-sitting/) and [light](/problems/seeing-light-in-sitting/) pages as convergent practice guidance; the attainment-framework claims registered without endorsement; the refusals kept as the episode's honesty feature.
