## Why this record matters

This is the archive's record of the teacher's **complete half-to-full-lotus training program** — the episode where the posture doctrine becomes a concrete method with concrete costs, and the episode the site most explicitly declines to follow. The site keeps it for three reasons: it documents the real demands of the traditional double-lotus path (diet, prostrations, daily pain-endurance, hip-opening stretches) in the teacher's own words; it is the strongest single source for the [posture question's](/experiences/questions/must-i-sit-cross-legged/) evidence file — the teacher's "four hours" and "half an hour of double is worth two of single" are exactly the claims the site's practice-layer answer does not adopt; and it contains the episode's most human moment — the correction to the baby's mother ("I thought you were a layman; you are a lady bodhisattva, my apologies") — which the archive records as the series' own instance of the teacher adjusting his line to the person, in sharp contrast with the [hard line of episode 25](/experiences/notes/exp-014-double-lotus-doctrine/).

## The record (paraphrased, anonymized)

- **The reports:** lying-down practice with eye congestion; single-lotus plateaus; stiff hips; a baby's mother practicing amid her children; thick thighs keeping the knees up.
- **The program, in its own terms:** no lying down; cushion the rear third of the hips; single-lotus as a preliminary only; four-hour sessions as the goal; meat first given up, then prostrations, then daily pain-endurance; soles-to-soles pulled to the perineum; knees to the ground an hour a day; forehead to the ground until breathing eases; then double-lotus; butterfly and frog stretches with graduated weights; eat less, move more.
- **The three layers, kept apart:** the reports, the program, and the alternative reading — the site's own answer (posture is a tool, not a requirement; the tradition's own graded system; mandating full-lotus is the fastest path to injury), the four-hour non-adoption, the lying-down physiology note, the convergent flexibility technique versus the injury-risky forced methods, and the lineage boundary-statement recorded without adjudication.
