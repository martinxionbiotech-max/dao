## Why this record matters

This is the archive's record of the series' **physiology map at its most explicit** — the complete channel circuit (kidneys → spine → Baihui → Renzhong → Danzhong → Dantian → Huiyin → kidneys), the prostration regime at its most extreme, and the teacher's own joke against displaying the attainments. It is kept for three reasons: it documents the series' first full-channel itinerary, which every later channel-reference in the series points back to; it preserves the episode's central health claim (one meal, 864 prostrations, three hours' sleep — "medical common sense does not apply") with the site's non-endorsement attached, as the [cure question](/questions/can-meditation-cure-illness/) requires; and it keeps the anti-display line — the [寒暑不侵] attainment demoted to "don't be a lunatic in a padded jacket" — which, with [EXP-020](/experiences/exp-020-false-first-dhyana/)'s demotion of the ecstatic report and [EXP-016](/experiences/exp-016-nine-dhyanas-map/)'s "I have not achieved it myself," forms the series' standing habit of cutting its own attainments down to size.
