**Anonymized note (paraphrased from a public forum thread).** A practitioner with an ongoing sitting habit reported that on several consecutive nights they would suddenly wake with a clench at the dantian, after which a felt 'qi' moved through the body — now here, now there — with heat at the back and profuse sweating, until they forcibly stopped the process.

**Why this note matters.** Night-time qi sensations are a recurring theme in Chinese sitting communities, and this report illustrates the interpretive fork documented throughout this site: the tradition reads such events as qi circulation stirred by practice; a modern reading notes that sleep-onset sensory events are common, and that daytime attention focused on the abdomen plausibly shapes what surfaces at night. The knowledge base's position: record both readings, assert neither, and flag the non-negotiable — repeated night sweats and sleep disturbance deserve medical evaluation regardless of interpretive framework.
