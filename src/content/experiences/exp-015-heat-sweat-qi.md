## Why this record matters

This is the archive's map of the **body's responses to double-lotus**, in the teacher's own taxonomy — and its centerpiece is the standing distinction between two inner motions: qi-sensation (guideable, take it to the dantian) and blood-flow sensation (unguideable, a sign of unclean blood). The site keeps the record because it documents, in one episode, how a living tradition's practitioners *read* their own bodies — heat as repair, sweat as purification, belly-waves as organ-adjustment, abdominal filling as a named stage — and because the record's three layers make the site's separation visible: the practitioner reports (warmth, spinning, sweat, pain, filling), the traditional map (repair, high-energy blood, expulsion, the two sensations), and the alternative reading (thermoregulation, digestive reflexes, hypnic-family phenomena, the non-adoption of the taxonomy's diagnostic claims). The convergence is real and kept: switch the legs, do not force, and expect the painful beginning — the same advice the site's own [protocol](/tools/sitting-protocol/) gives.

## The record (paraphrased, anonymized)

- **The reports:** the beginner's warmth-then-spinning with daytime clarity; the lying-down repeat that ends on exit; the double-lotus heat and perineum block; the profuse sweating; the daily noon headaches; the heat after clearing a thought; the spontaneous belly-waves with belching and flatulence; the two-month reporter's foot pain and the sensation of qi filling from lower to middle dantian with aching shoulders.
- **The teacher's map, in its own terms:** heat is repair — the first step; more oxygen, more alertness, less sleep; the worse the body, the more it sweats; the noon headaches are adaptation, not damage; the belly-waves are the organs adjusting — expel above and below, and the organs' bulk shrinks; the abdominal filling is a named stage ending in felt swelling; bone repair hurts and is endured; and the taxonomy: qi can be guided to the dantian, blood-flow sensation cannot — feeling the blood move means the blood is not yet clean, and one should feel neither; interior sensation itself is early warning, since the five senses chase the outside while the inside is ignored until the eve of a stroke; switch legs; observe nothing — use doubt-inquiry or thought-inquiry.
- **The three layers, kept apart:** the reports, the map, and the alternative reading (thermoregulation and digestive reflexes; the taxonomy as the tradition's, not anatomy's; the site's non-adoption; the convergences kept).
