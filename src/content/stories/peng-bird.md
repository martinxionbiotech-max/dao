## The story

In the Northern Deep there is a fish named Kun — so large no one knows how many thousand li it spans. It changes into a bird named Peng, whose back is likewise measureless, and when it rouses itself to fly, its wings are like clouds hung from the edge of the sky.

When the sea moves, the bird sets out for the Southern Deep — the Pool of Heaven. The *Qixie*, a book of marvels, records it: when the Peng travels south, it strikes the water for three thousand li, then spirals up on a whirlwind for ninety thousand li, riding the June winds. Mists and dust are living things breathing against each other; the blue of the sky — is it its true color, or distance without limit? Looking down, the Peng would see the same.

The book's own gloss follows, in the voice of physics: **if water is not deep enough, it has no strength to carry a great boat; pour a cup of water into a hollow and a mustard seed floats but the cup itself sticks** — the water too shallow, the boat too large. **If the wind is not thick enough, it has no strength to bear great wings.** Only at ninety thousand li is the wind under the bird at last, and only then does it ride the blue sky with nothing in its way.

## Framing: proportion as the first lesson

The book opens with size, on purpose. Three moves matter:

- **The scale is the argument.** Kun-Peng is not decoration; it is the tradition's first statement of *proportion*: the depth that carries, the wind that bears, the preparation that precedes the movement. "Riding" (培风 — to accumulate, to ride the wind) is the word later practice traditions will use for the conditions of sitting: the [tiaoxi](/practices/tiaoxi/) ladder and the [sitting protocol](/tools/sitting-protocol/) are both, in the end, water-depth and wind-thickness problems.
- **The skeptics are in the story.** The cicada and the dove laugh at the ninety-thousand-li journey — "I hop to the elm and back, what need is there to go so far?" — and the book answers them with the double-scale line (small knowledge / small years). The [three-in-the-morning](/stories/three-in-the-morning/) monkeys and these small birds are the same audience: the unexamined measure by which a short flight looks sufficient.
- **The change itself is the practice.** Fish into bird: the tradition's founding image is *transformation* (化), the same medium as the [drumming-basin](/stories/drumming-basin/) cosmology — no form fixed, every state a passage. The sitting traditions inherit the verb: the [zhizhi](/translations/daodejing-16/) "return," the [zuowang](/practices/zuowang/) "forgetting," are all this 化, slowed down and entered.

## Why it belongs in this knowledge base

Because it is the book's front door, and the front door says: **the practice is not the same size for everyone, and the conditions decide what can fly.** Every page of this site that warns against comparing one's sitting to another's — the [length question](/experiences/questions/how-long-should-i-sit/), the [problems archive](/problems/restlessness-in-sitting/) — is the Peng passage restated as policy.

**Uncertainty preserved:** the *inner* chapter opening (earliest stratum); the cosmology is poetic (Kun, the Pool of Heaven, the marvels book) — and the reading here is the traditional one, with the fable kept a fable.
