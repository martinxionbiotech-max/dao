## The story

The disciple of Robber Zhi asked: "Does robbery have a Way?" Zhi said: "Is there anywhere the Way is not? (何适而无有道邪) To guess from outside what is stored within the house — that is sagacity (圣). To enter first — that is courage (勇). To leave last — that is righteousness (义). To know what is possible — that is wisdom (知). To divide evenly — that is benevolence (仁). Never has there been one under heaven who lacked these five and became a great robber."

The chapter then does its arithmetic: the good man cannot stand without the sage's Way; Robber Zhi cannot act without it either. Since the good are few and the not-good many, the sage's Way benefits the world little and harms it much — so: the sage is born and the great robber arises; while the sage does not die, the great robber does not stop (圣人不死，大盗不止). And the chapter's sharpest line, kept by the whole tradition: the thief who steals a hook is executed, while the thief who steals a state becomes a lord — and where the lords' gates stand, the righteous gather (窃钩者诛，窃国者为诸侯，诸侯之门而义士存焉). It closes with the borrowed warning: 国之利器不可以示人 — the sharp tools of the state must not be shown.

## Framing: the tool-kit that serves whoever holds it

- **The five virtues, recited by the thief.** 圣勇义知仁 — the exact list the moralists teach — delivered in a robber's mouth, complete and correct: the satire is not that the thief is a hypocrite, but that he is a *graduate*. The virtues are content-neutral techniques (guessing, entering first, leaving last, knowing, dividing) and they make robbery *work*. The chapter's charge is the site's own [institutional wing](/stories/bole-horses/): like Bole's 治, the virtues are tools — and the chapter shows who else uses them.
- **何适而无有道邪 — the hijacked word.** "Is there anywhere the Way is not?" — the book's own [Way](/glossary/dao/), spoken by its arch-thief, and the chapter does not correct him. The Way in this chapter is available to anyone who practices it — which is exactly the book's claim elsewhere, and exactly the problem here: availability is not allegiance. The [tu-shan butcher](/stories/tushan-shuo/) used Chu's law *against* the king; Robber Zhi uses the sage's virtues *for* robbery. The same tools, two hands.
- **The escalation, stated as arithmetic.** The chapter's method matches its sister chapter's: [Bole counted dead horses](/stories/bole-horses/) (十二三 → 已过半); this chapter counts *who* benefits — 善人少而不善人多, so 利天下也少而害天下也多. Then the scale-law: 窃钩者诛，窃国者为诸侯 — theft is punished at the bottom and ennobled at the top. The line survives as the tradition's most quoted political arithmetic, and the site keeps it as the counter-page to [Cao Shang](/stories/cao-shang/) — the man who *bought* the lordship scale for a hundred chariots.
- **The five-missing-never.** 五者不备而能成大盗者，天下未之有也 — "never has one lacking the five become a great robber": the robber's *standard of excellence*, stated with the same grammar the moralists use. The chapter's target is not crime but the claim that virtue-list *possession* means anything about use. The tools do not choose; the hands choose.
- **The drift, registered.** 盗亦有道 today circulates as "even thieves have their code of honor" — a romanticized reading in which the thief has ethics. The chapter says something sharper: the thief has *the* ethics — the standard ones. The site registers the drift per its [idiom discipline](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/): the modern usage keeps the word and loses the accusation.
- **The chapter, declared.** Ch. 10 is an *outer* chapter (declared per the [layering rule](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/)) — the book's most systematic attack on the institutional use of virtue, one chapter after [Ma Ti](/stories/bole-horses/). Robber Zhi himself is the book's recurring arch-thief; this passage is his most quoted appearance.

Because this site's [source discipline](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/) is this chapter's question, asked of itself: what is a tool, and who is holding it? The practice this site studies — settling, returning, breathing — is also a tool-kit, and the chapter's warning applies to the site's own materials: the techniques serve whoever holds them, and the [experience archive](/experiences/) exists so that the reader can see the hands before trusting the tools.
