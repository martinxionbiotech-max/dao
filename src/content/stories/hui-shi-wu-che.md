## The passage

惠施多方，其书五车，其道舛驳，其言也不中。历物之意，曰："至大无外，谓之大一；至小无内，谓之小一。无厚，不可积也，其大千里。天与地卑，山与泽平。日方中方睨，物方生方死。大同而与小同异，此之谓小同异；万物毕同毕异，此之谓大同异。南方无穷而有穷，今日适越而昔来。连环可解也。我知天之中央，燕之北越之南是也。氾爱万物，天地一体也。"

Huishi had learning in many directions, his books five cartloads, his way mixed and impure, his words never hitting the mark. The meanings he worked through things: "The supremely great has no outside — call it the Great One; the supremely small has no inside — call it the Small One. What has no thickness cannot be piled up, yet it extends a thousand li. Heaven is as low as earth; mountains are level with marshes. The sun is at the middle and already declining; a thing is born and already dying. The great sameness differs from the small sameness — these are the small samenesses and differences; the ten thousand things are all the same and all different — these are the great samenesses and differences. The south is without limit and has a limit; going to Yue today means arriving yesterday. Linked rings can be unlocked. I know the center of the world: north of Yan, south of Yue. Love all things in flood; heaven and earth are one body."

And the verdict, after the disputers' twenty-one paradoxes (eggs have feathers; a chicken has three legs; a foot-long staff, halved daily, is never exhausted): 惠施不能以此自宁，散于万物而不厌，卒以善辩为名。惜乎！惠施之才，骀荡而不得，逐万物而不反，是穷响以声，形与影竞走也，悲夫！ — Huishi could not rest in this; he scattered himself among the ten thousand things without tiring, and ended with a reputation for skilled disputation. What a pity: Huishi's talent — free-running and never arriving, chasing the ten thousand things and never turning back, like chasing an echo by shouting, racing one's own shadow. Sad, is it not.

## Why this passage matters

- **The book pays its friend, then pays its verdict.** Huishi is the book's sparring partner — the [owl's prize](/stories/phoenix-and-owl/), the [fish debate](/stories/fish-happiness/), the [gourd's buyer](/stories/useless-gourd/) — and this, the final chapter of the whole collection, is where the book pays its dues twice over: first by *recording the man's system* (the ten theses are preserved verbatim — the book does its rival the honor of exact quotation, the same honor it gives its own [speech doctrine](/stories/zhi-yan/) elsewhere in the chapter), and then by the verdict: 其道舛驳，其言也不中 — wide, mixed, never landing. The site's [qiwu page](/glossary/qiwu/) holds the book's standing measure for such tournaments: a disputation that wins the mouth and loses the mind ([the debaters](/stories/three-in-the-morning/) are the same disease in miniature).
- **The paradoxes are a conversation, not a manifesto.** The chapter's grammar matters, and the site keeps it: the ten theses are Huishi's (历物之意); the twenty-one paradoxes (卵有毛 … 一尺之棰) are the *disputers'* answers to him — 辩者以此与惠施相应，终身无穷 — the debaters matched him with these, endlessly. The site registers the division because the tradition has a habit of dumping the whole list on Huishi's cart, which changes the genre: the ten are a system; the twenty-one are a tournament.
- **The verdict is an elegy, not a refutation.** 惜乎 — what a pity — is the chapter's closing note, and it is the same note the book struck once before, at Huishi's actual grave: the [plaster-shaver](/stories/yun-jin-cheng-feng/) story in which Zhuangzi, passing the tomb, says he has lost his partner — since the death of the man who could match him, there is no one left to talk to. The two pages together are the book's complete Huishi file: the living opponent it argued with, the dead friend it mourned. The verdict in this chapter is not contempt; it is the grief of a man watching talent run past every rest-stop — 逐万物而不反.
- **The drift to register.** The idiom dictionary is explicit (and the site records it per its [drift discipline](/glossary/qiwu/)): modern 学富五车, "learning rich as five cartloads," praises erudition — the *quantity* clause of the original sentence, with the verdict clause (舛驳, 不中) dropped. The book measured books by whether they landed; the modern language measures them by weight. The site's own [term policy](/tools/how-to-verify-sources/) is the original sentence restored: sources are weighed for what they establish, not counted for how many cartloads they fill.
