**Direct answer:** The review that defined the modern mechanistic account: mindfulness meditation's effects are proposed to run through **enhanced self-regulation**, assembled from three components — **attention control, emotion regulation, and self-awareness**. It is the most-cited neuroscience review of the field (Tang, Hölzel & Posner, *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*, 2015), and it is the page this site uses for the "what does the brain evidence actually support" question, alongside the [2014 meta-analysis](/research/mindfulness-meta-analysis-2014/) for clinical outcomes.

## The proposed mechanism, in its own terms

- **Attention control** — the ability to hold and redirect attention (the anterior cingulate and fronto-parietal networks); the practice component most directly trained by every sitting tradition, including the [jingzuo](/practices/jingzuo/) and [tiaoxi](/practices/tiaoxi/) anchor-holding documented on this site.
- **Emotion regulation** — the reduced reactivity and faster recovery studied through limbic-prefrontal pathways; the family of effects the [problems pages](/problems/restlessness-in-sitting/) translate into practical terms.
- **Self-awareness** — the changed relationship to experience itself; the deconstructive component, nearest in spirit to the [zuowang](/practices/zuowang/) family, and the one the [Schlosser 2019](/research/schlosser-2019/) survey flags as carrying the highest reported rate of unpleasant experiences.

## The caveats the authors themselves state

The paper's honesty is part of its value, and this page keeps it:

- **"The underlying neural mechanisms remain unclear."** The review proposes, rather than settles: the three-component model is a framework, not a measured chain.
- **"More methodologically rigorous studies are required."** Small samples, mixed practice types, and publication bias are named by the authors as open problems.
- **The practices studied are modern mindfulness programs** — mostly the standardized 8-week forms. None of the studies reviewed measure the Daoist practices this site documents (zuowang, shouyi, taixi). The mechanisms are *plausible neighbors*, not confirmations; the boundary the [comparison page](/comparisons/zuowang-vs-mindfulness/) draws applies here exactly.

## How this site uses the paper

The three-component frame appears across the site as *vocabulary for describing practice*, not as proof of it: attention control under the [holding-pole](/practices/shouyi/) pages, self-awareness under the [dropping](/practices/zuowang/) pages, emotion regulation under the [problems](/problems/) archive. The paper licenses careful description; it does not license causal claims about qi or the seven steps of [Sima Chengzhen](/people/sima-chengzhen/).

**Uncertainty preserved:** a narrative review, not new data; the mechanism is a proposal; and the gap between "mindfulness" as measured and "Daoist sitting" as documented is stated on every page that inherits this one.
