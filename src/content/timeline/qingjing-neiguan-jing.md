**Event:** Between the sixth and early seventh centuries CE, two short anonymous scriptures appeared that would define Tang meditation vocabulary: the *Qingjing Jing* (Scripture of Constant Clarity and Stillness, ~400 characters) with its Madhyamaka-influenced emptying ladder, and the *Neiguan Jing* (Scripture of Inner Observation) with its "still the spirit, settle the mind, no wild thoughts arise" method. Both are attributed to Taishang Laojun (the deified Laozi); scholarship dates the compositions to this window and notes Buddhist and Chongxuan influence.

**Evidence class:** received scriptures; composition dating is scholarly inference.

**Why it matters here:** these two texts are the direct sources of Sima Chengzhen's *Zuowang Lun* — the bridge from Han shouyi to Tang system. See the [Qingjing Jing](/texts/qingjing-jing/) and [Neiguan Jing](/texts/neiguan-jing/) pages.
