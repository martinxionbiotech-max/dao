**Event:** In the early eighth century, Sima Chengzhen — Shangqing patriarch, Tiantai recluse, and teacher of three Tang emperors — wrote the *Zuowang Lun*: seven stages from reverent faith through collecting the mind and true observation to attaining the Dao. Building on the *Qingjing Jing*, *Neiguan Jing*, and the *Zhuangzi*'s classical passage, it fixed zuowang as a named, teachable system. The treatise circulated at court (Xuanzong received him in 721) and entered the canon; the Song *Daoshu* would later record the tradition's own equation: "inner observation is what is called zuowang."

**Evidence class:** primary text; biographical context from official histories and steles.

**Why it matters here:** this is the pivot event of Chinese contemplative history — before it, zuowang was a famous passage; after it, a discipline. See the [treatise](/texts/zuowang-lun/) and [author](/people/sima-chengzhen/) pages.
