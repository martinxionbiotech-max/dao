**Event:** The *Taiping Jing* (Scripture of Great Peace), compiled in the Eastern Han, transmits the earliest sustained teaching on shouyi — guarding the One — defined as keeping jing, qi, and shen united within. Its practical passages include explicit exit conditions: if agitation arises during practice, leave the room rather than force the sitting.

**Evidence class:** received scripture with layered compositional history; Han material at the core (scholarly consensus), later accretions possible.

**Why it matters here:** this is the oldest documented account of a seated, non-forcing contemplative discipline with safety language built in — the ancestor of the later zuowang manuals and the anchor of this site's [shouyi](/concepts/shouyi/) and [restlessness](/problems/restlessness-in-sitting/) pages.
