## The event, in layers

The beginning of the tradition that made [cunsi](/practices/cunsi/) visualization a systematic art, date by date:

- **c. 364–370 CE — the sessions.** In the reign of Emperor Ai, at Jurong in the Jiangnan region, the young Celestial Master adept [Yang Xi](/people/yang-xi/) records nightly sessions in which — per the tradition's own account — the deified [Wei Huacun](/people/wei-huacun/) and other celestial figures descend and dictate scriptures, in the household of the official Xu Mi and his son Xu Hui. The claimed transmission: the *Shangqing* ("Highest Clarity") scriptures, thirty-one juan, written out in clerical script.
- **The corpus.** What the sessions leave behind becomes the Shangqing corpus — the body of revealed texts on interior visualization, the celestial bureaucracies of the body, and the ascent of the spirit — the source of the [jing-qi-shen](/glossary/jing-qi-shen/) body-map and the [Huangting Jing's](/texts/huangting-jing/) traditional attribution.
- **Fifth century — the first collection.** Gu Huan gathers the scattered records into the *Zhenji* ("Traces of the Perfected") — the compilation that makes a scripture-corpus out of household notes.
- **From 492 — the definitive edition.** [Tao Hongjing](/people/sima-chengzhen/)'s predecessor in the Maoshan line — Tao retreats to Maoshan and, searching out the Yang–Xu manuscripts, compiles the *Zhengao* ("Declarations of the Perfected") with annotations — the text through which the revelations reach all later generations, and the charter document of the Maoshan lineage (which later numbers Yang Xi among its earliest masters).

## Why this event matters to the site

Because it is the site's hinge between **the Zhuangzi's century and the meditation manuals' centuries**. The early sitting tradition (the [Zhuangzi](/texts/zhuangzi/) passages, the [Taiping Jing](/texts/taiping-jing/) instructions) flows into the Shangqing corpus here — and out the other side come the [visualization practices](/practices/cunsi/), the [inner-scenes scripture](/texts/huangting-jing/), and, through the Maoshan line that [Sima Chengzhen](/people/sima-chengzhen/) later heads, the [Zuowang Lun](/texts/zuowang-lun/) itself. The site's two practice poles — [filling](/practices/cunsi/) and [emptying](/practices/zuowang/) — both pass through this event. It is also the site's clearest instance of the **revelation-as-compilation** pattern: the same structure the [Kou Qianzhi timeline](/timeline/kou-qianzhi-reform/) documents in the north — a person, a claimed descent, a textual corpus, an institution — here in the south, one generation earlier.

**Uncertainty preserved:** the descents are the tradition's account, not historical events; the sessions' exact dates and the division of labor between Yang Xi and the Xu scribes are the tradition's record; the page dates the *documents* (the corpus, the collections) and marks the *claims* (the descents) as claims.
