**Event:** In the *Shiji* (Records of the Grand Historian, completed c. 100 BCE), Sima Qian recorded the first surviving biographies of Laozi and Zhuang Zhou: the archivist who departed west after writing "five thousand and some characters," the dragon-praise of Confucius, and the lacquer-garden clerk who refused a Chu kingdom. The chapter itself flags the Laozi account as uncertain — "some say he was Lao Laizi... some say he was the historian Dan... the world does not know which is right."

**Evidence class:** primary text (c. 100 BCE), recording traditions then already old. The biographical content is traditional, not verified.

**Why it matters here:** this is the documentary baseline for every later Laozi and Zhuangzi biography. See the [Laozi](/people/laozi/) and [Zhuang Zhou](/people/zhuang-zhou/) pages.
