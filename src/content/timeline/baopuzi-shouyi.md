**Event:** Around 320 CE, Ge Hong (283–343) codified the shouyi tradition in the *Baopuzi neipian* ('Dizhen' chapter): "guarding the One" and "guarding the True One," practiced in a quiet chamber, with graded signs of attainment claimed in the text's own hagiographic register.

**Evidence class:** primary text (~320 CE). The attainment signs are traditional claims recorded by the source, not verified reports.

**Why it matters here:** with Ge Hong, shouyi stops being scattered scripture teaching and becomes a named, numbered discipline with a recognized lineage — the institutionalization step between the *Taiping Jing* and the Tang zuowang manuals. See the [shouyi](/concepts/shouyi/) page.
