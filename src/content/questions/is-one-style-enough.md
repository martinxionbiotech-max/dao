**The short answer:** one style at a time — that is the consistent answer of both the archive's teacher and the site's classical sources. But "one style" is not the same as "one tradition," and the site keeps the two questions apart: the teacher's rule governs how you *practice* (pick one method, go deep, don't switch mid-sitting), while the site's [combining page](/experiences/questions/combine-zuowang-mindfulness/) asks a different question about *traditions* (can the Daoist and the mindfulness frames be paired at all). Mixing up the two is the most common misreading of both pages.

## What the sources say, layer by layer

- **The archive-teacher layer: the diagnosed error.** The [EXP-027 file](/experiences/notes/exp-027-finding-a-teacher/) preserves the case: a reader who recited the Dizang sutra for two years, then sat torn between three methods — mind-watching, the Guanyin ear-root method, concentrating spirit into the qi-cave — switching between them in a single sitting. The teacher's diagnosis is structural, not moral: jumping from book to book, trying each "supreme" method for a few days, wastes the best time — practice has no shortcut, only hard work. And the teacher's own teaching is itself the model of the rule: one program (sitting, breathing, prostrations, recitation) held without variation across the whole series, with new questions answered by *reading what was already written* before asking. The site's [progress page](/experiences/questions/what-counts-as-progress/) holds the companion rule that explains why switching is self-defeating: progress is measured in months of the same practice, and a switched method resets the measurement.
- **The source-text layer: the One, from both books.** The [Daodejing's ch. 10](/translations/daodejing-10-shouyi/) opens with the book's own practice-formula (载营魄抱一，能无离乎 — carrying the soul and embracing the One, can you keep them from parting?), and [ch. 22](/translations/daodejing-22/) makes it the sage's method (圣人抱一为天下式 — the sage embraces the One as the world's pattern). The site's [shouyi pages](/practices/shouyi/) hold the tradition this formula became (the Taiping Jing's 守一, the Baopuzi's one-guarding), and the site's [cicada-catcher story](/stories/cicada-catcher/) holds the Zhuangzi's version of the same instruction in one clause: 用志不分，乃凝于神 — where the intention does not divide, it condenses into spirit. Both books say the same thing with different machinery: one point, held.
- **The boundary: pairing is not switching.** The site's [combining page](/experiences/questions/combine-zuowang-mindfulness/) and its [choosing page](/experiences/questions/cunsi-or-zuowang/) already draw the line the archive's teacher also draws in his own way: *mixing traditions for study is legitimate; mixing methods inside one sitting is the error*. The archive's teacher has no objection to a practitioner who does one method for two years and then asks about another — the objection is to doing three in one sitting. The site's pages agree: the [choosing page](/experiences/questions/cunsi-or-zuowang/) is about deciding *before* you sit, not during.

## The two honest declarations

- **What the site does not require.** The site does not require the archive's one-program curriculum (that is a Buddhist monastic frame, registered per the site's [boundary](/experiences/notes/exp-013-buddhist-daoist-boundary/), not adopted), and it does not claim one-method practice is safer or more effective in any modern-research sense — no such research is cited anywhere on this site. The one-style rule is a *practice-tradition* rule, stated plainly as such.
- **What the site does require.** The description discipline, as always: if you practice one style, say which; if you switch, say that too — and know the difference between the two claims the pages actually make. "One style at a time" is the archive's answer to the method question; "traditions can be paired" is the site's open question about frames. Both are on the site, and they do not contradict each other — they answer different questions, and the [breath episode](/experiences/notes/exp-023-breath-is-the-key/) shows the archive's teacher giving the same two-part answer in practice: one program, held for years.
