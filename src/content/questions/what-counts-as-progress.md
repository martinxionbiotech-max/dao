**The short answer:** there are three different things called "progress," and they are not interchangeable: the *stage* (the tradition's ladder), the *process marker* (what you can actually check — attendance, breath rate, sitting time, daily-life reactivity), and the *symptom outcome* (what research measures). The site's line, stated on the [results page](/questions/how-long-until-results/): measure the practice, not the stage — and the stage-claims, whether yours or a teacher's, are the tradition's map, not your transcript.

## The three registers, kept apart

- **The stage register: ladders that disagree.** The [Zuowang lun](/texts/zuowang-lun/) has seven stages; the Buddhist record the site's [EXP-016](/experiences/exp-016-nine-dhyanas-map/) preserves has nine dhyanas; the series' own teacher draws a line the texts don't — [nothing before the channels open counts as meditation at all](/experiences/exp-022-its-all-theatre/), only sitting. The ladders are real, and they are *different systems*: the site's standing rule is that no stage-language converts between traditions, and no practitioner is obligated to accept any ladder. If your progress question needs a stage answer, it needs a *chosen* ladder first — and the choice is optional.
- **The process register: what you can actually check.** The teachers the site archives give exactly this kind of marker: a breath rate (12 to 4-6 per minute, [EXP-023](/experiences/exp-023-breath-is-the-key/)); a sitting duration that holds without the leg giving out ([EXP-018](/experiences/exp-018-halflotus-to-full-lotus/)); the legs alternating rather than one side hardening ([EXP-024](/experiences/exp-024-standing-and-sitting/)); the surfacing of old material treated as normal ([EXP-004](/experiences/exp-004-emotional-surfacing/), [EXP-024](/experiences/exp-024-standing-and-sitting/)); attendance itself — "no meal eaten once keeps you full forever." These are checkable from the outside, and the site holds them as the honest currency of progress: the practice, measured in days and breaths.
- **The symptom register: what research measures.** The [eight-week evidence](/research/mindfulness-meta-analysis-2014/) shows mood and pain outcomes move — measurable, modest, symptom-level. Nothing in the literature measures a dhyana or a channel-opening; the site's [qi question](/questions/qi-belief-necessary/) already drew this line. Progress at this layer is real and is the *only* layer an outside observer can verify — and it says nothing about the stages.
- **The negative markers: what is NOT progress.** The record the site archives is unusually specific here, and the site keeps all of it: drowsy blur is [not stillness](/patterns/drowsiness-vs-stillness/); a "special state" that turns out to be [hypoxia-laced haze](/experiences/exp-022-its-all-theatre/) is not entry; the "first dhyana" that [collapses under questioning](/experiences/exp-020-false-first-dhyana/) was not a first dhyana; and the site's own [feeling-nothing page](/questions/i-feel-nothing-when-i-sit/) covers the commonest false negative — nothing happening is not nothing happening. The shepherd's rule from [the two deaths](/stories/shanbao-zhangyi/) is the classical form of all of it: progress is whipping the sheep that lags, not admiring the one that runs.
- **The one asymmetry to hold.** You may check your own process markers freely — the tradition itself encourages exactly this ([量练习不量境界](/questions/how-long-until-results/)). But no one — including a teacher — may *certify your stage* for you, and the series' own teacher models the refusal: claims are met with ["not yet"](/experiences/exp-016-nine-dhyanas-map/), not with certificates. Progress, on this site, is the practice's own record; attainment is the tradition's map.
