**The short answer:** they are not rivals; they are **two poles of one path, used in sequence**. The tradition's own texts make the choice for you: *build first, then dissolve.* [Cunsi](/practices/cunsi/) constructs the inner map — the dantian, the palaces, the spirits — giving the scattered mind something organized to hold; [zuowang](/practices/zuowang/) then lets the whole construction go. The Baopuzi even states the economy version: among thousands of visualization methods, guarding the One makes the rest unnecessary — *one* thing held instead of the whole library, which is the midpoint between filling and emptying. This question is therefore answered at the level the sources answer it: the relationship is documented; the personal choice (which school, which teacher, which sequence) is yours to make with the conditions stated.

## What the sources actually say

- **The Zhuangzi's own sequence.** The heart-fasting passage begins *by unifying* — "unify your intention" (一志) — before it lets go: first the single point, then the emptiness ("the Dao gathers only in emptiness," ch. 4, full translation [here](/translations/xinzhai-passage/)). The text never lets you skip the holding phase.
- **One chapter, both engines.** The Dasheng chapter holds the concentration-pole stories ([cicada-catcher](/stories/cicada-catcher/), [Artisan Qing](/stories/artisan-qing/)) and the subtraction-pole story ([wooden rooster](/stories/wooden-rooster/)) side by side, without choosing — the editorial [here](/blog/three-craftsmen-dazheng/) maps the two-engine reading.
- **The Baopuzi's ranking.** "Knowing the One completes the ten thousand affairs": the guarded single point is offered as the *replacement* for the full visualization curriculum — one holds or one empties, but one does not run the whole library. The minimal-practice reading of the tradition is the tradition's own.
- **The Huangting's role.** The [Huangting Jing](/texts/huangting-jing/) is the visualization tradition's map-document; it presupposes a curriculum, a teacher, and years — and it is *the* text that shows why cunsi is not beginner-simple: the images must be learned correctly before they can be used or released.

## A practical reading, stated as editorial synthesis

- **If you sit alone, without a teacher:** hold, don't build — the guarded anchor (breath via [tiaoxi](/practices/tiaoxi/), or the One via [shouyi](/practices/shouyi/)) is the pole the tradition's minimal versions recommend, and it is the one this site's [sitting protocol](/tools/sitting-protocol/) follows.
- **If you sit in a lineage that teaches cunsi:** the images come with the lineage's map and checks; the same tradition expects them eventually to be released, and its texts say so.
- **Both poles share one boundary:** neither is a medical treatment, and neither promises its claims as measured fact — the boundaries each page states separately still apply to the combination.

**Uncertainty preserved:** the *relationship* between the poles is documented in the sources; the *recommended personal sequence* (which order, how long) is lineage-specific and oral — this page gives the documented geometry, not a curriculum.
