**The question:** is belief in qi — the traditional vital-breath physiology — necessary to practice, or to benefit from, the sitting traditions this site documents?

## What the texts themselves require

- **The *Zhuangzi* ch. 4 ladder mentions qi, but the instruction is operational:** "listen with the qi" is presented as a *receptivity* below naming — the text's own gloss is that qi is "empty and waits for things." A secular reader can treat this as a model of attention without accepting a physiology.
- **The *Qingjing Jing*'s program is psychological first:** dispel desire → mind stills → spirit clears. No qi-belief is demanded at the entrance.
- **But the tradition as a whole is not belief-neutral:** the *Neiguan Jing* and later inner-alchemy material embed the practice in qi-and-spirit anatomy; the *Taiping Jing* shouyi is defined as guarding jing, qi, and shen. Full participation in the traditional program includes the traditional physiology.

## What the research literature can support

- **Family-level evidence:** seated meditation of the mindfulness type shows modest benefits for anxiety, depression, and pain **without any qi-model** (Goyal 2014) — evidence that settled sitting can benefit, in the measured outcomes, independent of traditional metaphysics.
- **What it cannot support:** nothing in the literature tests qi-models themselves, nor whether belief in them changes outcomes. The research is silent on the physiology question.

## What transfers without belief

The components that do not depend on the qi-model: sitting quietly, the subtraction logic, the non-forcing counsel, the attention-ladder model, the simplification-of-life stages. These are the elements this site's [sitting protocol](/tools/sitting-protocol/) compiles.

## What remains unanswered

Whether practicing *with* the qi-frame produces different or additional effects compared with practicing without it is an open empirical question — no study compares framed vs. unframed Daoist sitting. The honest answer state: **open**, with a defensible interim position: the frame is optional for the procedural components, non-optional for full traditional participation, and untested either way.
