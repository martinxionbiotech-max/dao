**The short answer:** no — feeling nothing is not failure. It is the baseline the tradition's own texts describe, and the most common honest report of settled sitting. The measure is **continuity, not intensity** — and the tradition's single best sentence for the state is [ch. 6's](/translations/daodejing-06/) 绵绵若存: *thread-thin, as if existing* — not vivid, not absent.

## What "nothing" usually is

Three different states hide inside "I feel nothing," and the site's records show all three:

- **Plain settling.** Attention, untrained, cannot yet perceive the subtle level — the 绵绵 level sits below the threshold of vividness. The tradition's answer is that this is *already the practice*: the [quiet sitting](/concepts/jingzuo/) tradition never promised warmth; it promised [stillness](/concepts/xu/). The sensation reports in the [experience records](/experiences/) are *reports*, not *requirements* — and the same records contain plenty of quiet, nothing-special sittings, which no one flags because they are not problems.
- **Boredom — the mind's demand for entertainment.** This is the actual content of most "nothing": the mind, deprived of stimulus, reports deprivation as failure. The tradition's word for the remedy is the same as for everything else: stay. The [what-is-stillness question](/experiences/questions/what-is-stillness/) holds the full answer; the short form is that boredom is the mind's first honest response to being left alone, not evidence the sitting is broken.
- **Dullness — real drowsiness wearing boredom's clothes.** The [drowsiness-vs-stillness pattern](/experiences/patterns/drowsiness-vs-stillness/) holds the site's method for telling them apart (collapse into sleep vs. the sustained, thread-thin continuity), and the [falling-asleep question](/experiences/questions/falling-asleep-during-meditation/) handles the sleep side.

## The boundary the site enforces

- **No target experience is required.** The [qi-belief question](/experiences/questions/qi-belief-necessary/) already answers the belief side: you do not need to believe in qi, and you do not need to *feel* it. Nothing-happening is a complete record of a sitting, not an incomplete one.
- **Do not chase the warmth.** The [warmth-tingling question](/experiences/questions/warmth-tingling-when-sitting/) and the [seeing-light page](/problems/seeing-light-in-sitting/) enforce the same discipline for sensations: chasing them is how practice turns into grasping — and the tradition's own texts call the ungrasped state the goal, not the obstacle.
- **The one real measure.** 绵绵若存 — continuity, as if existing. If the sitting held a thread of attention that neither collapsed into sleep nor exploded into agitation, it was a sitting. Intensity was never on the page.

**Not medical advice.** Persistent anhedonia, emotional numbness, or loss of interest that extends beyond sitting should be discussed with a professional — stillness-feeling is not the same as depression-feeling, and this page only speaks to the former.
