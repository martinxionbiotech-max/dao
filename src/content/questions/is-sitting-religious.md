**The short answer:** the *core instructions* are not religious — you can practice the settling, the breathing, and the returning without adopting any belief — but the *tradition* is: its texts frame the practice with soteriological claims (immortality, the dhyana stages, the channel system) that cannot be separated from the practice by the texts themselves, and which no secular source the site trusts has verified. The honest line: practice the core freely; treat the frame as optional metaphysics, not as the price of admission — and never let anyone tell you the frame *is* the core.

## The three registers, kept apart

- **The textual register: the core has no god.** The site's [fasting of the mind passage](/translations/xinzhai-passage/) is taught by Confucius to Yan Hui — the tradition's own founding practice scene stars a non-Daoist; the [opening of the Qiwu Lun](/translations/qiwulun-wu-sang-wo/) is a leaning sit, a sigh, and a question about the self; the [Zuowang lun](/texts/zuowang-lun/)'s seven stages ask for trust, stability, and forgetting — no deity appears in the instructions themselves. The core is a technology of attention, and the texts know it: what they invoke is [the Way](/glossary/dao/), which the site's [creator question](/questions/does-daoism-believe-in-a-creator/) has already shown they refuse to personify.
- **The historical register: the practice grew in temples.** What the core does not require, the institutions supplied anyway: the [Five Pecks of Rice](/people/zhang-daoling/) parishes organized the earliest communities, the [zhaijiao rites](/people/lu-xiujing/) gave the practice its liturgy, the [Shangqing revelations](/people/yang-xi/) gave it its scriptures, and the [Neo-Confucians](/timeline/zhuzi-yulei-jingzuo/) gave it its secular-but-serious armchair. The site registers this honestly: the practice you are doing was preserved by people who believed things you may not believe — preservation does not require belief, but the record would not exist without it.
- **The practical register: the boundary is the claims.** The [comparison page](/comparisons/zuowang-vs-mindfulness/) has already made the split: the transferable part (settling, returning, breathing) has modern secular descendants that [work, measurably, for symptom outcomes](/research/mindfulness-meta-analysis-2014/); the non-transferable part (immortality, dhyana stages, channel-opening) has no such record — and the site's standing rule applies: absence of evidence is not disproof, but it is the absence of a reason to require belief. The [qi-belief question](/questions/qi-belief-necessary/) applies the same line to the one claim practitioners most often think they must accept.
- **The one asymmetry to hold.** You may practice the core without the frame — the texts themselves show this is possible (Confucius, the Neo-Confucians). But you may not *claim the frame's results* without the frame's burden: the soteriological claims are religious claims, tested nowhere, and the site's [experience pages](/experiences/) archive them as the tradition's testimony, not as fact. Practice like a Confucian; claim like a cataloger.
