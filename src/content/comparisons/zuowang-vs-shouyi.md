## The two poles

- **Zuowang** ([concept](/concepts/zuowang/) · [practice](/practices/zuowang/)) — "sitting in oblivion": the method of *subtraction*. Drop the body, drop perception, drop the constructed self (堕肢体，黜聪明，离形去知，同于大通). The image is emptying; the direction is letting go.
- **Shouyi** ([concept](/concepts/shouyi/)) — "guarding the One": the method of *holding*. Carry the soul, embrace the One (载营魄抱一), keep attention fixed on the unity of essence-qi-spirit, in the Taiping Jing's concrete chamber protocol. The image is concentration; the direction is holding steady.

## The comparison, from the sources

| Dimension | Zuowang | Shouyi |
|---|---|---|
| Root text | Zhuangzi ch. 6; Zuowang Lun (Tang) | Daodejing ch. 10; Taiping Jing (Han); Baopuzi (4th c.) |
| Core verb | 忘 forget; 堕 drop; 离 depart | 守 guard; 抱 embrace; 存 hold |
| Relation to content | Release mental content | Fix attention on one content (the One) |
| Relation to the body | Forget the limbs and body (堕肢体) | The body is the vessel held in order (chamber protocol, proper posture) |
| Image | Empty vessel, dark mirror | Lamp held steady; "knowing the One completes the ten thousand affairs" (知一则万事毕) |
| Failure mode | Forgetting becomes dull sinking (昏沉) | Guarding becomes strain and fixation |
| Later systematization | Sima Chengzhen, seven stages | Internal alchemy's one-pointed attention; the "holding" lineage through the tradition |

## What the tradition itself says about their relationship

The tradition does not treat them as rivals, and neither does this site. The **Baopuzi** is explicit: among thousands of protective visualizations, "if you know the way of guarding the One, all of these can be set aside — knowing the One completes the ten thousand affairs" — shouyi as the *economical* method. And the Zhuangzi's own geometry runs the other way: guard nothing; the One is what remains when everything else is dropped ("唯道集虚" — only the Way gathers in emptiness, ch. 4). The two routes converge from opposite ends: **guarding empties by exclusion; forgetting empties by release** — the Dazheng chapter's two engines of training, documented on the [three-craftsmen editorial](/blog/three-craftsmen-dazheng/).

## Practical reading

- **Beginners tend to start at the shouyi end** — a held point (the breath, the dantian, the One) gives the scattered mind something to hold; this is why nearly every tradition starts with a one-pointed anchor.
- **The zuowang end is the classical terminus** — but reached *through* the held point, not instead of it: the Zhuangzi's fasting begins by uniting the intention (一志) before it lets everything go (ch. 4, [translation](/translations/xinzhai-passage/)).
- **The combined reading this site recommends:** guard until stable, then forget. Shouyi builds the concentration zuowang spends; zuowang spends what shouyi built. That reading is stated as *editorial synthesis* — the sources give the pieces, not the combination.

**Uncertainty preserved:** both concepts' classical definitions are well attested; their systematic relationship is the tradition's own later construction, recorded here as such.
